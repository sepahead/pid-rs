# Authorship and citation

**Sepehr Mahmoudian** is the author of the first-party pid-rs reports, mathematical
expositions, publication figures, and software. Current report PDFs identify him
on the first page and in the PDF Author field.

Cite a report by its title, Sepehr Mahmoudian, its stated year, and the exact
repository commit or release. Use [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) for the software.
Cite the defining PID papers separately; project authorship does not transfer
authorship of those methods or establish priority for a mathematical result.

Older first-party PDFs in `audit/review-packets/`, `audit/evidence/`, and publication
test fixtures retain their original bytes because recorded reviews and negative
results identify those exact files. Their generic labels, such as “pid-rs project
analysis” and “pid-rs contributors”, refer to Sepehr Mahmoudian. Use the current
report in `output/pdf/` when citing current results. Embedded figure PDFs are parts
of their parent reports, not separate papers.

Third-party papers, licenses, and externally generated review reports retain their
stated provenance. This notice does not reassign their authorship.
