## Mathematical and numerical scope

The ordinary mathematical argument bounds the radial logarithmic derivative of a positive atom. It also proves continuity and strict increase of the least error over all directions. Its inputs and conclusions can be checked without an optimizer. A separately assigned model derived the same result using squared amplitude as its variable before reading the first proof. Both derivations used the same coefficient identities and underlying population. A common model family, author and host limit their independence. Neither argument is a stock-kernel proof.

An exploratory floating-point search suggested a crossing near 0.340315168725 and a useful direction near 0.21787956. It did not prove anything about other directions or numerical rounding. They share the problem, its selected inputs and a common implementation author. These finite predicates grant no empirical holdout or novel-in-the-literature claim.
