The general reference must enumerate all nonempty antichains of three nonempty-mask sources from scratch and construct OR-of-AND event products from every complete row with integer weights. Recover atoms by finite-poset triangular subtraction in multiplicative form in stable keys. Derive the specialized products separately from the declared coarse-to-fine marginals and split independent per level. No approximate sign tests. Cover all 6 source permutations, a constant coarse source, a repeated adjacent partition, a constant or independent target, a nonuniform target with a negative local signed increment, a dataset with two incomparable source partitions and reversed-order misuse. Each accepted chain must match 108 keyed values, include a nonzero net atom at each permitted level in at least one case over the campaign, and show equality across the other nodes. Reject nonintegral or nonpositive counts, duplicate canonical rows, unequal target weights counted as equal, and an input with no rows. Finite thresholds and success formatting do not supply kernel or statistical credit. Neither candidate nor checks may change the mathematical object or the frozen ordering. Fixed budget 1 GiB, 90 seconds per route (Python 3.14 ordinary and optimized, one thread). Print the totals and full keyed rational products, include the original raw inputs, explicit adverse outcomes and SHA-256 of each result. A missing check or arithmetic mismatch is a failed attempt. The source and data will be read separately from any proposer return. Keep actual failures, and register a different attempt if the predicate changes.
