# Independent derivation for categorical sources ordered by deterministic coarsening

The structural reduction occurred during this reading. This is an ordinary model-assisted derivation for this question. It has no preregistered-discovery, empirical or formal-kernel status.

Take three finite sources and a finite target with any joint probability law. Assume a permutation permits labels X1,X2,X3 in increasing resolution such that X1 is a deterministic function of X2 and X2 of X3 almost surely. This applies to the joint support, and requires no independence, uniformity or deterministic target. Equal partitions and constant sources are allowed. For the exact integer certificate take the law of the positive occupied counts c_z/N; a repeated measurement is represented through its count. The canonical format does not thereby permit duplicate serialized state keys.

Fix a supported complete row z, and let F_j be the event that the jth source equals its value at z. On positive support F3 is contained in F2, which is contained in F1. In the MGW finite categorical OR-of-AND construction, a branch matching a nonempty subset B has event F_{max B}; an antichain α has event F_{d(α)}, where d(α)=min_{B in α} max B. Conditional marginals use the same event intersected with the actual target of z. All of these masses and their keyed target intersections are positive. This also says exactly what goes wrong if the partitions are incomparable.

Let L_j be the antichain of one-element sets {s} for each s≥j. Then d(α)≥j iff each branch B of α contains some s≥j, equivalently L_j precedes α in the published redundancy order. With coarse, middle and fine as sources 1,2,3, the keys are L1=01+02+04, L2=02+04 and L3=04. Applying the source permutation before formatting the stable keys is necessary. Local cumulative values depend only on d(α). For any of the three component tables, write v_j(z) for its cumulative value at level j and v_0=0. The identity v_{d(α)}=sum_j [L_j precedes α]·(v_j-v_{j-1}) directly constructs the atoms. Zeta inversion on the finite partial order makes them unique. The same holds after joint-law averaging. In particular, all 15 other nodes have zero atoms for each component, giving 45 structural zero atom coordinates. A tie between two adjacent partitions gives zero increment at the corresponding node. Any reordering of equal partitions leaves the total assignment invariant. The 54 cumulative coordinates can repeat levels but generally are not zero.

Put X0 constant. After averaging, positive, negative-component magnitude and net cumulatives at level j are H(Xj), H(Xj|T), I(Xj;T). Subtracting consecutive values gives H(Xj|X_{j-1}), H(Xj|T,X_{j-1}) and I(Xj;T|X_{j-1}), respectively. Determinism of X_{j-1} from Xj, and the finite entropy chain rule, give these identities. All three averages are nonnegative for these nested source partitions. A negative local net increment remains possible where a finer source lowers the conditional probability of the observed target. Zero average conditional mutual information means conditional independence on supported conditioning events. A negative exact averaged net atom rules out this chain geometry for this method and exact law. The converse does not follow. The finest source determines their full tuple; summing all net increments gives I(X3;T).

For positive integer counts write t_z for the target count, u_j(z) for the count matching Xj, and w_j(z) for the count matching Xj and T. At level j the exact positive-component rational product is P_j^+=product_z (N/u_j(z))^{c_z}, the negative-magnitude product is P_j^-=product_z (t_z/w_j(z))^{c_z}, and the net product is P_j^+/P_j^-. Take each level-zero product to be 1. Every named cumulative product is its level product. Every atom product at L_j is P_j/P_{j-1}, and each of the other 45 atom products is exactly 1. Taking natural logarithms divided by N produces all 108 values in nats.
