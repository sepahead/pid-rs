# Fixed question for an empirical failure of deterministic refinement

For a positive finite count table of paired categorical readings, let N denote the number of observations including repetitions. At a row z, let C(z) be the fibre of its first reading and F(z) the fibre of its second reading. Define the informative deficit as the empirical average, with observation weights, of log(|C(z) union F(z)|/|C(z)|), in nats. This is the difference between two named informative cumulatives of the MGW construction. Which strictly positive lower bound, if any, is sharp at fixed N when the second reading does not determine the first on the observed table? Characterize attainability and the equality conditions only to the extent proved. The target is immaterial. An empty sample is outside the domain; give a statement for N=1,2 and larger counts.
