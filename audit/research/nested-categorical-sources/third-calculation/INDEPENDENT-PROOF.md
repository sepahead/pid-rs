# Independent derivation on the original three-source finite categorical carrier

Fix a nonempty table of positive integer counts, its total N, and its empirical mass c/N on each occupied joint row of (X1,X2,X3,T). All source and target states are exact labels. On the occupied support assume X1=f(X2) and X2=g(X3). X1 is the coarsest partition. The target is arbitrary. All assertions below use that finite common distribution and natural logarithms. The source is Makkeh, Gutknecht and Wibral, arXiv:2002.03356v5, equations (6) and (13)–(15). The complete atom and product conventions are those in claims/SX-CERTIFIED-AVERAGED-PID3-001/conventions.md. The reduction, coarsening test and fixed-total gap below are my derived statements about this specification. No statistical population-identification or priority claim follows.

1. At a supported key z let E_i be the event of matching Xi. On this support E3 is contained in E2, which is contained in E1. The intersection for a nonempty source mask a is then the event E_j with j=max(a), using source numbers 1 through 3. Taking the union over a nonempty antichain alpha gives E_k, where k=min over all a in alpha of max(a). The informative, misinformative and signed cumulatives at that node equal H(Xk), H(Xk|T), and I(Xk;T), respectively. This follows by averaging -log P(E_k) and -log P(E_k|T=t) with the original row-count weights. No division by a null row is needed.

2. Set g1={{1},{2},{3}}, g2={{2},{3}}, g3={{3}}. Under the original order g_j precedes alpha exactly when every mask in alpha contains at least one index >=j. This is equivalent to k>=j. For any of the three components write H_j for the corresponding one-source value and set H_0=0. The cumulative function is therefore the sum over j=1..3 of (H_j-H_(j-1)) times the indicator that g_j precedes alpha. The zeta relation between cumulatives and atoms gives these three increments at g1,g2,g3 and zero at all other nodes. In the original serialization their stable keys are 01+02+04, 02+04 and 04, with zero-based indices 16,10,3. This avoids conflating the specialized Rust and certificate orders. The increments are H(X_j|X_(j-1)), H(X_j|X_(j-1),T), and I(X_j;T|X_(j-1)), using a constant X0. This last identity uses the stated deterministic nesting. Thus all 45 products for 15 absent atom nodes are exactly 1. For each of the remaining nine atom coordinates the product is the ratio of consecutive one-source products, with initial product 1. The 54 cumulative products are the corresponding three one-source products at their assigned levels. These formulas determine all 108 exact rationals. Some or all of the remaining products can also equal 1, for example when all variables are constant. The count construction and log identities alone do not specify the numerical interval encoding or validate a new program.

3. Take any two sources (X,Y), without a nesting assumption. Write C_X^+ for the informative cumulative on the singleton X and C_{{X},{Y}}^+ for the informative cumulative on the union of the two singletons. For each occupied (x,y), write a_x, b_y, n_xy for its X margin, Y margin and joint count. The per-row difference is log[(a_x+b_y-n_xy)/a_x], always nonnegative. The average is zero exactly when each observed Y label occurs at just one X label. This is exactly X=f(Y) on the empirical support. Comparing only source entropies does not give this result: two independent fair bits have the same entropy without determining one another. Two correctly oriented comparisons test both edges in the three-source chain.

4. Define Delta=C_X^+-C_{{X},{Y}}^+. For a failed map and N>=2 let m=floor(N/2), n=ceil(N/2). I obtain the sharp universal inequality
   exp(N Delta) >= ((m+1)(n+1))/(mn).
   Indeed choose a Y label shared by distinct coarse labels x1 and x2. Their whole margins are positive integers A,B with A+B<=N, and both joint counts are at least one. Each of the two affected rows contributes to exp(N Delta) a factor at least (1+1/A) or (1+1/B); all other factors are at least 1. The product decreases when either margin increases. With fixed sum N its value is 1+(N+1)/(AB), minimized by the most balanced integer margins. To attain it, use binary X with margins m,n, one shared Y label with count one at each X, and put any remaining counts on Y labels exclusive to their own X. The occupied table has at most four rows and at most three Y labels. All other factors are exactly one. This covers N=2 as well and can use a constant target and third source. For N=1 a failed map is impossible. In nats the sharp value is (1/N) log of this rational. As N grows this gap tends to zero. A sharper restricted-alphabet rule could hold if the specified Y alphabet cannot realize this witness. The result is about recognizing a failed map on this observed table. The margin does not treat changes in support or unknown sampling distributions, and cannot prove a map on unseen states.

The independent finite checker reconstructs the order and all original 108 products directly over a 16-cell nonuniform table and the constant singleton, compares integer products without logs, tests the biconditional and inequality over 2x3, 3x2 and 3x3 small count tables and exercises the attaining family up to N=256.
