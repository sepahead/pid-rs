necessity, current status, or provenance of any proposition it mentions. In particular:

  exclusions, Ehrlich continuous shared exclusions, KSG mutual-information arithmetic, and the
  project-defined finite-binary64 PID2 constructor remain distinct objects.
- Exact-real algebra, correctly rounded represented-input arithmetic, compiled binary64 behavior,
  finite corpus evidence, estimator sampling behavior, and population/application validity are
  separate edges.
- A finite exhaustive domain is only as broad as its declared carrier. A model-requested count,
  witness, digest, theorem, or mutation inventory has zero evidence credit until independently
  reconstructed.
- Two proof tools can share one mistranslation. A formal kernel result covers only its exact
  statement and trust surface; it cannot close source correspondence, runtime refinement, or
  statistical validity by implication.
- Current successors listed in `INDEX.json` are navigation targets. Their existence does not make
  them semantically equivalent to these prompts or transfer evidence between revisions.
