# Retained worktree fragments, 13 November 2026

Sepehr Mahmoudian · 2026

Status: **inert historical text archive**. The [index](INDEX.json) identifies 95 exact byte
images, totaling 6,899,066 bytes, recovered from two retained pid-rs worktrees. This archive
preserves research notes, counterexamples, exposed descriptive data, proposed checks and failed
publication history. It does not adopt their scientific claims or execute their code.

## How to read the archive

Every payload filename ends in `.txt`, including former Markdown, Rust, Python, shell, JSON,
SVG and TeX files. Every payload has Git mode `100644`. The index records its original relative
path, filesystem mode, source HEAD, length and SHA-256 digest. Contents are unchanged; relative
links and imperative wording inside them are historical text. An archived `AGENTS.md.txt`,
`README.md.txt`, handoff, changelog, proposal or success statement has no current authority.
References to PDFs and raw logs do not mean that this packet supplies those artifacts.

The two source HEADs identify the bases of the retained dirty worktrees; they do not claim that
Git at those commits contained the pending bytes. Exact pending-file recovery was checked
separately before packaging. Local custody records remain private. This packet covers its
95 enumerated payloads and one license notice, not the containing worktrees, their ignored state,
full Git metadata or every historical research store.

## Exclusions and review limits

Twenty-two raw execution captures, host-path build records and unqualified PDFs from the two
pending-file inventories remain outside this public packet. The separate July provider-withheld
advisory companions remain withheld. Their exact local preimages were retained; a local copy or
hash is not an approved restricted off-host deposit. No whole dirty worktree is cleared for
deletion by this archive. NCP is a separate project and was excluded from filesystem work;
existing mentions in five pid-rs payloads remain inert historical context.

The review checked every selected byte image and parsed the derived JSON and SVG text. It was
model-assisted, with shared host, sources and custody; it is not human or institutional
independence, a line-by-line mathematical audit, a proof of secrecy, a legal opinion or a formal
acceptance decision. The manifest binds bytes, not authenticity or scientific truth.
