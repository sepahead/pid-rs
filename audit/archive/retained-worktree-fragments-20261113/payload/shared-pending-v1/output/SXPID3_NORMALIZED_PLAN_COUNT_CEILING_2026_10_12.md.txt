# A sharp count ceiling for normalized SxPID3 certificate plans

12 October 2026. Project-derived resource theorem; hand/source reviewed.
Formal verification, implementation and a matching qualified PDF remain open.

## Question and useful result

Which empirical count tables can satisfy the existing exact-product policy
before any large integer power is formed?

If at least two complete source triples occur, this normalization requires
**total count at most 87,380**. The bound is sharp for all 108 product plans.
It is not a sample-size limit of PID or a statistical accuracy guarantee.

## Object, assumptions and symbol key

The defining information measure is categorical shared exclusions from
[Makkeh, Gutknecht and Wibral (MGW), arXiv:2002.03356v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.03356v5).
Use the repository's [source map](../claims/SX-CERTIFIED-AVERAGED-PID3-001/source-correspondence-v4.md),
[conventions](../claims/SX-CERTIFIED-AVERAGED-PID3-001/conventions.md) and
[resource policy](../claims/SX-CERTIFIED-AVERAGED-PID3-001/bindings.md).
The plan policy and ceiling below are project-specific, not a theorem claimed
by that paper.

| Symbol or term | Meaning and domain |
|---|---|
| \(S_1,S_2,S_3\) | Three ordered sources, each taking values in a nonempty finite categorical alphabet |
| \(T\) | Target in a nonempty finite categorical alphabet |
| \(z=(s_1,s_2,s_3,t)\) | One occupied complete source-target cell |
| \(c_z\) | Positive integer count of cell \(z\); unlisted cells have count zero |
| \(N\) | Positive total count, \(\sum_z c_z\) |
| \(s\), \(n_s\) | Complete source triple and its count summed over all target values |
| \(a/b\), \(e\) | Reduced positive rational base and its nonzero signed integer exponent |
| \(\operatorname{bitlen}(a)\) | Number of binary digits of positive integer \(a\); for example, \(\operatorname{bitlen}(1)=1\), \(\operatorname{bitlen}(2)=2\) |
| \(C\) | Projected cost of one normalized product plan |
| \(Z,M\) | Frozen incidence matrix and its inverse; \(Z\) maps atom values to cumulative values and \(MZ=ZM\) is the identity matrix |
| Unit product | Exact rational product equal to one; its logarithm is zero |
| Antichain | A family of nonempty source subsets, with no distinct member containing another |

Each count table defines the empirical probability \(c_z/N\). Computing this
functional requires no independence, Gaussian, or population-sampling model.
Information values use natural logarithms, in nats. No theorem transfers here
to continuous Ehrlich PID, another PID, or confidence coverage.

A source event requires agreement on every source in at least one antichain
member: AND inside a member, OR across members. Hexadecimal keys identify
subsets: \(\mathtt{01}=\{1\}\), \(\mathtt{03}=\{1,2\}\),
\(\mathtt{05}=\{1,3\}\), and \(\mathtt{07}=\{1,2,3\}\).
A plus sign between keys separates alternative members.

There are 18 three-source lattice positions. The 108 expressions comprise
cumulative and atom expressions, each split into informative, misinformative,
and signed-net components: \(18\cdot2\cdot3=108\). They are not 108 independent
atoms.

## Normalization to which the theorem applies

For every whole rational factor: reduce by greatest common divisor; discard
units; orient the numerator above the denominator, negating the exponent for
a reciprocal; combine equal oriented bases; remove zero exponents; then sort.

Do not prime-factor, collapse the whole product first, divide the input counts
by their common divisor, or cancel the final \(1/N\) averaging weight. Such a
different representation needs its own declared policy and proof.

For the remaining factors, the unchanged cost rule is

\[
C=2+\sum_{\text{factors }a/b}|e|
  \bigl(\operatorname{bitlen}(a)+\operatorname{bitlen}(b)\bigr).
\]

The per-coordinate ceiling is 262,144; the aggregate ceiling over all 108
coordinates is 8,388,608. Each plan also permits at most 8,192 factors and
absolute exponent at most 1,048,576. These are admission rules, not measured
instructions or memory bytes.

## Proof of the necessary ceiling

Let \(F\) denote the full-source informative cumulative at key \(\mathtt{07}\):

\[
F=\sum_z\frac{c_z}{N}\ln\frac{N}{n_{s(z)}}
 =\frac1N\ln\prod_z\left(\frac{N}{n_{s(z)}}\right)^{c_z}.
\]

Here \(s(z)\) selects the three source values in cell \(z\); \(F\) is in nats.
All bases and weights concern the same complete joint empirical law.

1. If two distinct source triples occur, every occupied cell satisfies
   \(0<n_{s(z)}<N\). Thus every displayed base exceeds one.
2. After reduction, its numerator is at least two and its denominator at
   least one. Their binary-digit sum is therefore at least three.
3. Every exponent is positive. Orientation leaves it positive; merging equal
   bases preserves the sum of exponents, which is \(\sum_zc_z=N\).
4. Consequently this coordinate has \(C\ge2+3N\).
5. Admission requires
   \(2+3N\le262144\), hence
   \[
   N\le\left\lfloor\frac{262142}{3}\right\rfloor=87380.
   \]

At \(N=87381\), the lower cost is 262145, already too large.
This gives a necessary refusal before powers. It does not guarantee admission
below the ceiling: aggregate, parser, event-work, memory and interval checks
remain separate.

## Sharpness across all 108 plans

Put \(K=43690\) and \(N=2K=87380\). Use two occupied source triples,
\((0,0,0)\) and \((1,0,0)\), each with count \(K\).
Only \(S_1\) varies; \(S_2,S_3\) are constant.

Exactly five antichains require \(S_1\) in every member:

\[
\mathtt{01},\quad\mathtt{03},\quad\mathtt{05},\quad
\mathtt{07},\quad\mathtt{03+05}.
\]

Their event count is \(K\), so each informative cumulative product is
\((2/1)^N\). Every other antichain has a member omitting source 1.
That member always holds, so its event count is \(N\) and its product is one.

The five-event indicator vector is column \(\mathtt{01}\) of \(Z\).
Applying \(M\) leaves only atom \(\mathtt{01}\), since \(MZ\) is the identity.
All nonunit bases equal \(2/1\), so inverse coefficients cancel at the
normalized-plan level, before powers.

Two target choices give distinct checks:

| Target law | Why the other component behaves this way | Nonunit coordinates |
|---|---|---|
| \(T=0\) on both rows | Target count is \(N\); target-restricted event counts equal source-event counts. Misinformative duplicates informative; signed net is zero. | Five informative and five misinformative cumulatives, plus their two atoms at \(\mathtt{01}\) |
| \(T=S_1\) | Each target class has count \(K\) and fixes the source triple. Every event holds inside it, so all misinformative factors are one. Net duplicates informative. | Five informative and five net cumulatives, plus their two atoms at \(\mathtt{01}\) |

Both laws have 12 nonunit and 96 unit plans. Each nonunit plan has one factor
\(2/1\), exponent \(N\), and cost \(2+3N=262142\). A unit plan costs two.
Therefore the aggregate is

\[
12(2+3N)+96\cdot2=216+36N=3145896<8388608.
\]

The term and exponent caps also hold. Two rows, five-digit counts and a
17-bit total fit the count-format bounds. This proves sharpness of the
**complete normalized product-plan policy**, not end-to-end certification.
The first law has zero net information; the second has joint mutual
information and signed atom \(\mathtt{01}\) equal to \(\ln2\) nats.
Neither is evidence of predictive improvement.

## Negative construction: the aggregate matters

Let all three sources copy the same balanced bit, with constant target.
Every antichain event has count \(N/2\). All 18 informative and 18
misinformative cumulative products are nonunit; their two bottom atoms are
also nonunit. All net products are one.

There are 38 nonunit and 70 unit plans. At the same \(N=87380\),

\[
38(2+3N)+70\cdot2=216+114N=9961536>8388608.
\]

Each coordinate fits its ceiling, but the aggregate fails. Keep this negative
control. Omitting components, raising the ceiling, or silently rescaling
counts does not repair the original-policy failure.

## Constant-source exception and practical meaning

If the complete source triple is constant, every source event holds.
Its count is \(N\); its target-restricted count equals the target count.
All cumulative, atom and net products are one, even if the target varies.
All 108 empty plans cost two each, giving aggregate 216. Other input and
resource rules still apply; the nonconstant-source ceiling does not.

Replicating all counts preserves the empirical probabilities and PID values,
but scales nonunit exponents. It can therefore change certificate admission.
This is a representation cost, not a limitation of the PID functional.

The immediate use is an early refusal check and two exact boundary controls.
For a real dataset, it warns that the current certification policy cannot
admit a nonconstant table above the ceiling. It supplies no neural, robotic,
world-model or deployment gain.

## Rust implementation and computational cost

The existing default-build APIs are
`stable::categorical::discrete_sxpid3` and the general
`discrete_sxpid_n` family. Their empirical-PMF, binary64
implementation is not this exact-product certifier.
See [Rust implementation and cost](../RUST_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_COST.md) and
[the implementation](../crates/pid-core/src/sxpid.rs).

The early ceiling guard is proposed, not implemented or compiled.
For \(m\) occupied complete cells and at most \(W\) token/byte equality work
per source triple, testing constancy against the first triple costs
\(O(mW)\): at most a constant times \(mW\), with constant additional
borrowed-anchor storage. This excludes
input parsing, count summation, grouping and the later all-108 plans.
It changes neither the PID nor its sampling assumptions. It must refuse
without partial certification, before powers; cooperative cancellation and
allocation handling still need an implementation contract.

No training, model inference, preprocessing or resampling is performed.
No timing or measured memory is reported. Projected product bits are not
Python/Rust heap bounds or real-time guarantees.

## Evidence, process and remaining work

AI assisted direct count algebra and independent-first critique.
A floating PID run or MI-only comparison cannot establish this policy ceiling;
direct integer reasoning is simpler than a model benchmark for this question.
There was no empirical best-of-N selection or held-out application test.

The root derivation and an independent-first model critique separately
obtained the ceiling and different sharpness laws. The subsequent exposed
review checked both laws and the failed aggregate example. Eleven distinct
routes and 55 hostile lenses were recorded as coverage, not correctness votes.
They share the original MGW interpretation and project normalization policy.

This is hand/source evidence, not external human review, a kernel proof,
native execution, Rust refinement, scientific-priority or application evidence.
The retained critique requested no mathematical correction.
[All 18 component formulas](SXPID3_COMPONENT_COUNT_FACTORIZATION_2026_10_12.md)
remain a separate hand/source result.

Formalization, implementation, parser/resource/interval qualification and a
matching qualified PDF remain open. The original SxPID3 Programs A-E remain
0/5 closed under their unchanged [closure ledger](../RESEARCH_RESULTS_2026_10_10.md#closure-ledger--original-criteria-not-substitute-milestones).
