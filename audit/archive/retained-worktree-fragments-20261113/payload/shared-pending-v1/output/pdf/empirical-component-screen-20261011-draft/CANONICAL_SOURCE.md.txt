# Exact component bounds on two occupancy datasets

Sepehr Mahmoudian · 11 October 2026

## Result and question

Can a cheap, hand-derived bracket give a tightly controlled approximation to
categorical shared-exclusions components on real sensor count laws?

The fixed screen completed. Every one of 3,924 local reference intervals and
504 averaged reference intervals is contained in its bracket. Of 2,085 strictly
positive local components, 313 meet a four-parts-per-million relative midpoint
ceiling. The important negative result is that **none of the 184 strictly
positive averaged components meets that ceiling**. Only 21 meet 1%.

This identifies selective local numerical applicability, not a measured
prediction, control or sensor-placement benefit. A wide bracket is a valid but
unhelpful approximation; it is not a counterexample to the bracket theorem.
The study is exposed and descriptive, not untouched confirmation or a
scientific-priority claim. The [complete records and exact sources](data/component-bound-screen-20261011/STATUS.md)
retain every zero and losing case. A matching qualified PDF remains open.

## Defining measure and assumptions

The defining measure is Makkeh–Gutknecht–Wibral (MGW) categorical pointwise
shared exclusions, with its informative and misinformative components and
signed difference. It is not I_min, continuous shared exclusions, or a new PID.
The defining paper supplies the measure; the bounds and this screen are
repository derivations/compositions, with no scientific-novelty claim.
[MGW, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.103.032149),
[exact arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.03356v5).

Here is the complete input/output contract:

- A law is the finite empirical probability mass function from one frozen
  evaluation partition. Its row count is $N$; each occupied joint state has
  positive integer count $c$, and all such counts sum to $N$.
- Source variables $X_1,X_2,X_3$ are the inherited, training-fitted equal-width
  labels for light, carbon dioxide and temperature, in that order. Each alphabet
  is $\{0,1,2,3\}$. These labels are not raw lux, ppm or degrees Celsius.
- Target $T$ is occupancy absence/presence for UCI 357; occupant count
  $0,1,2,3$ for UCI 864; or a separate transformed presence target
  $1\{T>0\}$ for UCI 864. The latter means one when count is positive, zero
  otherwise. Target transformation changes the estimand.
- Joint support lies in at most 128 states for a binary target or 256 for a
  four-label target. Only occupied cells are stored; zero cells are implicit.
  There are 109 occupied anchors across fourteen overlapping evaluation laws.
  Three training laws receive schema validation but are not studied.
- Outputs are both nonnegative local components at all eighteen ordinary
  three-source antichains, their within-law averages, and signed net intervals.
  Natural logarithms give nats. No negative net atom is clamped.
- Rows need not be independent for these count-law identities. No independent-
  and-identically-distributed (IID),
  stationarity, population-support or confidence-coverage assumption is asserted.
  Temporal/day/block overlap prevents treating laws as independent replications.
- No input, quantizer, partition or scientific criterion was refitted after the
  computation. Historical raw-data processing and predictor/loss subtrees are
  inherited, not freshly scientifically validated by this screen.

The real problem is room occupancy estimation. UCI 357 describes timestamped
binary occupancy ground truth and environmental sensors. UCI 864 describes
manually recorded occupant counts and distributed sensor readings at thirty
second intervals. Neither description validates this analysis's encodings or
estimator. [UCI 357](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/357/occupancy%2Bdetection),
[UCI 864](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/864/room%2Boccupancy%2Bestimation).

## Events and the positive component

Fix an occupied anchor $(x_1,x_2,x_3,t)$. Lowercase letters are its fixed source
and target labels; uppercase letters are variables. For another row, the agreement set

\[
B=\{j\in\{1,2,3\}:X_j=x_j\}
\]

contains the source positions matching the anchor. An antichain \(\alpha\)
is a nonempty family of nonempty source collections, none contained in another.
Its event \(E\) occurs if **some** collection in \(\alpha\) has **all** of its
positions in \(B\). Thus branches are joined by OR; positions inside a branch
by AND. This avoids substituting a different redundancy event.

For one component use a probability law \(Q\): either the source marginal
\(P(X_1,X_2,X_3)\) for the informative component \(p^+\), or the target-conditioned
law \(P(X_1,X_2,X_3\mid T=t)\) for the misinformative component \(p^-\).
The signed atom is \(p^+-p^-\), not either nonnegative component alone.

Define \(u=Q(E)>0\). The excluded agreement sets form a family closed under
taking subsets. Its maximal members are \(m_1,\ldots,m_k\), where \(1\le k\le3\).
Define \(a_i=Q(B=m_i)\): one exact agreement cell, **not** the mass of all its
subsets. Positivity of the occupied anchor guarantees \(u>0\) in both laws.
The [full derivation](POSITIVE_CATEGORICAL_COMPONENT_BOUNDS_2026_10_11.md)
explains how these maximal cells localize the ordinary inverse row, including
the bottom's virtual certain event. That virtual event is not a nineteenth
ordinary atom.

Let \(r_i=a_i/u\), \(S=\sum_{i=1}^k r_i\),
\(V=\sum_{i=1}^k r_i^2/12\), and \(G=(k-1)!\prod_{i=1}^k r_i\).
The symbol \(!\) denotes factorial. The exact component is \(p=\ln R\), where

\[
R=\frac{\prod_{D\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\},\ |D|\text{ odd}}
(1+\sum_{i\in D}r_i)}
{\prod_{D\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\},\ |D|\text{ even}}
(1+\sum_{i\in D}r_i)}.
\]

Here \(D\) ranges over index subsets and \(|D|\) counts their members;
the empty subset contributes the factor one. No logarithm is evaluated in
binary64 to produce a certificate.

## Bracket: all steps and the meaning of its error

Introduce auxiliary independent uniform variables \(Y_1,\ldots,Y_k\) on
\([0,1]\). They express an integral; they are not assumptions about the sensors.
With \(Z=\sum_i r_iY_i\), the positive box-integral identity gives

\[
p=G\,\mathbb E[(1+Z)^{-k}],\qquad
0\le Z\le S,\quad \mathbb EZ=S/2,\quad\operatorname{Var}(Z)=V.
\]

Expectation \(\mathbb E\) and variance here refer only to these auxiliary
uniforms. For \(f(z)=(1+z)^{-k}\), direct differentiation gives

\[
\frac{k(k+1)}{(1+S)^{k+2}}\le f''(z)\le k(k+1),\qquad 0\le z\le S.
\]

Taylor expansion around \(S/2\) bounds its remainder between these curvature
extremes times \((Z-S/2)^2/2\). The expected linear term vanishes. Multiply
by \(G\), and define the midpoint-rule term \(J=G/(1+S/2)^k\). Then

\[
L=J+\frac{Gk(k+1)V}{2(1+S)^{k+2}},\qquad
U=J+\frac{Gk(k+1)V}{2},\qquad L\le p\le U.
\]

If any \(a_i=0\), the box integral is exactly zero: \(L=U=p=0\).
Otherwise \(L>0\). Define the reported approximation \(C=(L+U)/2\)
and ceiling \(\varepsilon=(U-L)/(2L)\). Because \(p\ge L\) and
\(|C-p|\le(U-L)/2\),

\[
\frac{|C-p|}{p}\le\varepsilon.
\]

The fixed, inclusive screens are \(\varepsilon\le1/100\) (1%) and
\(\varepsilon\le1/250000\) (four parts per million). Four ppm implies
1%, so there are only three possible positive-component outcomes: four ppm;
1%-only; or too wide for 1%. A zero has no relative error denominator and is
reported separately, not as a precision success. These intervals control
numerical approximation of the empirical functional, not sampling error.

For a law's average, weight each occupied anchor's endpoints by \(c/N\)
and sum. Recompute the relative ceiling from those averaged endpoints; do
not average local relative ceilings. Signed intervals cross-subtract:
\([L_+-U_-,\ U_+-L_-]\), with subscripts identifying the two components.
No signed relative-error screen is used because cancellation can make the
net arbitrarily small or zero.

## Independent-form reference computation

The helper independently reconstructs the eighteen antichains, verifies both
integer inverse products, and compares each exact maximal-cell product \(R\)
with the whole ordinary inverse-row product. This is a distinct source route,
not independent hardware, models, arithmetic or a Rust refinement.

For the mathematical logarithm, write \(R=2^e h\), with integer
\(e\ge0\) and rational \(1\le h<2\). For each argument \(b\in\{h,2\}\)
set \(z=(b-1)/(b+1)\). Thus \(0\le z\le1/3\). Define

\[
\ell(z)=2\sum_{j=0}^{63}\frac{z^{2j+1}}{2j+1},\qquad
d(z)=\frac{2z^{129}}{129(1-z^2)}.
\]

The summation index is \(j\); \(\ell\) is a finite rational sum and \(d\)
its geometric upper-tail bound. Positive omitted terms establish
\(\ln b\in[\ell(z),\ell(z)+d(z)]\). Combine the intervals using
\(\ln R=e\ln2+\ln h\), then widen exactly by integer floor/ceiling to a
grid with denominator \(2^{128}\). This encloses the mathematical logarithm;
it does not certify floating-point outward rounding.

Containment, disjoint conflict and unresolved overlap are separate outcomes.
An overall completed status would allow unresolved overlap; root therefore
recounted the actual flags. Here all local and averaged reference intervals
are contained, with zero conflicts and zero unresolved reference overlaps.
The general tail/box arguments remain hand/source reviewed, not newly
kernel-verified by this computation.

## Worked pair: the target-conditioned ratio matters

These explanatory cases were selected after observing the output. In the
earlier UCI 357 law, \(N=2665\). The bottom antichain is
\(\alpha=\{\{1\},\{2\},\{3\}\}\): at least one source matches. The only
maximal excluded pattern is the empty agreement set. Hence \(k=1\), and
for target-conditioned probabilities the misinformative component is
\(p^-=\ln(1+r)\). Both examples have the same source labels \((0,0,1)\).

For \(t=0\), the joint anchor count is 1303 and the target has 1693 rows.
Of those, 1671 match at least one source and 22 match none. Thus

\[
u=1671/1693,\quad a_1=22/1693,\quad r=22/1671,\quad p^-=\ln(1693/1671).
\]

For \(k=1\), substitute \(G=S=r\) and \(V=r^2/12\) to obtain

\[
J=\frac r{1+r/2},\quad
L=J+\frac{r^3}{12(1+r)^3},\quad U=J+\frac{r^3}{12}.
\]

Exact rational evaluation gives

\[
L=\frac{160136704123}{12243007762311},\quad
U=\frac{153974784205}{11771900975853},\quad
\varepsilon=\frac{19001307053806}{67924672054748201223}<\frac1{250000}.
\]

For \(t=1\), the joint anchor count is only two and the target has 972 rows.
Now 204 match at least one source and 768 match none. Therefore

\[
u=204/972,\quad a_1=768/972,\quad r=64/17,\quad p^-=\ln(81/17),
\]

and the same formulas yield

\[
L=\frac{105247936}{78121827},\quad U=\frac{4154560}{722211},\quad
\varepsilon=\frac{13209534464}{8079423587}>1.
\]

Both intervals contain the exact-reference interval. Only the first is a
near-exact midpoint approximation. Small absolute counts do not guarantee
small \(a_1/u\); target conditioning and event mass determine the useful
regime. The second anchor's small averaging weight does not allow removing it
or claiming that every averaged bound is narrow. These are components, not
the signed bottom atom and not classifier error.

## Rust implementation and computational cost

The stable `pid_core::stable::categorical::discrete_sxpid3` route computes
empirical categorical MGW atoms; it needs no experimental feature. The fitted
quantized wrapper is a composition for the frozen quantized estimand. The
current rational bound/reference screen is a Python research calculation,
not an implemented Rust interval API or formally proven Rust refinement.
No Rust estimator was executed in this screen. See the
[implementation/cost baseline](../RUST_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_COST.md).

Given \(u,a_1,\ldots,a_k\), the bracket takes \(O(k)\) rational arithmetic
operations; the notation describes growth in \(k\), not fixed machine time.
Extracting masses, building/checking the ordinary inverse, exact logarithm
references and output serialization are additional costs. The observed census
has 6,758 given-mass units, 14,388 cover factors, 7,194 odd/even factor pairs,
6,903 source equality comparisons and 2,301 target equality comparisons.
The carrier has eighteen nodes and its inverse 324 entries. Reference cache:
1,411 misses and 2,513 hits under a 4,096-entry cap. Fraction numerator,
denominator and greatest-common-divisor bit costs matter; no Rust or asymptotic
speedup is measured by these counts.

One stock CPython 3.14.6/Darwin execution in each mode recorded direct-child
fork-to-wait4 times of 890,104,791 ns (normal) and 877,622,333 ns (optimized),
and terminal direct-child resident-memory peaks of 42,696,704 and 42,762,240
bytes. This includes initialization and serialization. It is not a repeated
benchmark, authenticated hardware comparison or aggregate process-tree RSS.
No preprocessing, training, inference or resampling was performed here.

The unchanged helper's limits are 1 MiB input, 8 MiB stdout, 256 MiB observed
RSS, 180 seconds, fourteen laws and at most 256 anchors. Native stage timeout
is 185 seconds. Missing output, failed status, invalid schema and disjoint
reference conflicts cannot pass. Checks use explicit refusals, not assertions,
and remain active under `-O`. Cooperative resource checkpoints and captured
direct-child usage are not hard memory quotas or universal signal safety.
This is an offline screen; online or real-time suitability is unestablished.

scientific comparisons, final selected hashes and resource censuses. Twelve
separately observed capture faults test a finite engineering contract. Source

query. Its receipt and original unrun runner remain preserved; the new route
captures direct-child stock `wait4` usage, with its own prior authority and
failure controls. The scientific helper, input and criteria were unchanged.
The separate [component exposition PDF](pdf/component-bounds-20261011-draft/STATUS.md)
failed its mandatory raw-log equality check; that failure is not waived here.

Useful next work is a declared fallback from a wide cheap bracket to a more
accurate certified evaluation, followed by a matched task comparator if the
diagnostic is meant to inform sensor retention. Approximation accuracy alone
does not demonstrate utility. Neural/imaging and embodied/world-model studies
still require justified variable mappings, actual observed outcomes and
dependent-sample uncertainty. The [world-model decision](WORLD_MODEL_PID_DECISION_2026_10_11.md)
keeps generated forecasts separate from actual futures.

SxPID3 A–E, original finite formal obligations, angular and hyperbolic
qualification, statistical calibration, real task benefit and v1 remain open.
This bounded numerical screen is complete; those programs are not. No new
