# Small conditional MI need not mean stable PID allocation

12 October 2026. Sepehr Mahmoudian.

CO2 collapse changes an old occupancy PID allocation by about 0.9114 nats,
despite conditional MI about 0.00156 nats: a rounded-data hand comparison,
not a new experiment, certificate, recommendation or gain.

## Question and assumptions

Does little additional target information guarantee little change in the
signed PID explanation after one input becomes constant?

PID means partial information decomposition; MI means mutual information.
Use categorical Makkeh-Gutknecht-Wibral (MGW) shared exclusions, not continuous
Ehrlich PID or I_min. The [v5 definition, Section II](https://arxiv.org/html/2002.03356v5)
uses inclusive-OR source agreement for redundancy. The
[old study](../audit/evidence/real-occupancy-sensors-example-2026-09-08.md)
retains maps, counts, full results and negative predictors.

[UCI357, Candanedo (2016)](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/357/occupancy%2Bdetection)
records environmental inputs with photo-derived binary occupancy, CC BY 4.0.
Keep its original target. No population, causal or replication inference.

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| X, C | Light and CO2 labels in {0,1,2,3}; raw units lux and ppm before binning |
| T | Original occupancy bit, 0 or 1; x,c,t denote supported labels |
| N | 9752 later datatest2.txt rows |
| p | Complete empirical law of (X,C,T), each row weighted 1/N |
| q | Replace C by label 0 on EVERY row; preserve (X,T) and weights |
| R, U_X, U_C, S | Averaged signed redundancy, unique light, unique CO2 and synergy |
| I_X, I_C, I_XC | Shannon MI I(X;T), I(C;T), I((X,C);T) |
| D | Sum of absolute p-versus-q changes in the FOUR signed atoms |
| CMI | Conditional MI I(C;T given X), equal to I_XC-I_X |
| H(C), ln | Shannon source entropy and natural logarithm; information units nats |

Four equal-width bins were fitted on 8143 training rows only, then held fixed.
Interior ties go upward; boundary clamping retains out-of-range rows.
The nominal alphabet is 4x4x2, with 16 occupied cells in this recording.
No noise, scaling, filtering or target recoding. Zero cells and dependence
are allowed. Empirical arithmetic needs no independent, identically distributed rows.

## Every calculation step

The existing two-source reconstruction, on the SAME law, is

\[
U_X=I_X-R,\quad U_C=I_C-R,\quad
S=I_{XC}-I_X-I_C+R.
\]

At a supported q outcome, C=0 is certain. Its MGW event
(X=x OR C=0) is certain, also conditional on the target t.
The target probability stays unchanged, so its posterior/prior ratio is 1:
local redundancy ln1=0.
Averaging gives redundancy 0. The constant source has MI 0; appending it to
X leaves I_X unchanged. Thus q's atoms in order (R,U_X,U_C,S) are
(0,I_X,0,0).

Subtract q from p: (R,U_X-I_X,U_C,S)=(R,-R,U_C,S). Hence

\[
\boxed{D=2|R|+|U_C|+|S|.}
\]

This exact finite-law identity concerns signed averaged atoms, not local
values, all 108 three-source expressions, task loss or physical failure.

Old displayed atoms:
(0.276238458,0.204781420,-0.178691053,0.180249065) nats.
Using ONLY these rounded decimals:

1. I_X=R+U_X gives 0.481019878.
2. 2|R| gives 0.552476916.
3. Add |U_C|: 0.731167969.
4. Add |S|: D approximately **0.911417034 nats**.
5. CMI=U_C+S gives -0.178691053+0.180249065:
   approximately **0.001558012 nats**.

These rounded sums are not executed PID or an outward enclosure.
Do not clamp the negative atom: cancellation is essential.

Exact control under a DIFFERENT law: X=C=T copy a fair bit, assigning
probability 1/2 to each of (0,0,0) and (1,1,1). Each agreement event has mass
1/2 and conditional mass 1, so R=ln2. Each MI is ln2, giving
p atoms (ln2,0,0,0), then q atoms (0,ln2,0,0).
Thus D=2ln2 but CMI=0; X predicts T perfectly. Here ln2 means the natural
logarithm of 2. This collapse keeps T fixed; physical removal may not.

## Meaning, losing predictor and unrun follow-up

CMI concerns extra target information; D concerns this measure's allocation.
Neither determines sensor utility, acquisition cost, causal role or safety.
The old trained pair lost on later log loss: 0.050762 versus light's 0.047443
nats/row, lower better. Earlier results differ and remain in the full study.
The MGW-OR-inspired forecast lost on all three exposed recordings.
No new training, PID-guided selection or SOTA claim follows.

The [conditional bound](SXPID_ALLOCATION_L1_BOUND_2026_10_12.md) D<=4H(C)
gives an MGW allocation guarantee from entropy under published component
positivity. This example did not evaluate that ceiling's tightness.

An UNRUN three-source proposal retains light and collapses CO2/temperature as
a JOINT tuple, not this example's single C. Keep each original target:
357 binary; [864, Singh and Chaudhari (2018)](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/864/room%2Boccupancy%2Bestimation)
manual counts 0-3, CC BY 4.0. Do not pool laws.
[Recorded counts](data/constant-channel-occupancy-20261012/counts.json) show
seven 864 dates, not the provider's four-day description: the old exactly-four-
date protocol must refuse. Compare allocation/entropy/CMI with fixed and
retrained predictor loss on both collections. Useful nonconstant passes are
unobserved; no new experiment or qualified application.
