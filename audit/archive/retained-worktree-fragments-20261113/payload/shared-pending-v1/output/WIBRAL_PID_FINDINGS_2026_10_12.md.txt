# Wibral shared-exclusions PID: findings and evidence status

Sepehr Mahmoudian · 12 October 2026

No priority claim. SxPID3 A–E remain **0/5 closed**;
continuous, angular and hyperbolic qualification remain open.

## Measure and assumptions

The measure is finite categorical shared-exclusions PID, defined by Makkeh,
Gutknecht and Wibral (MGW), *Introducing a differentiable measure of
pointwise shared information* (2021),
[defining paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.103.032149).
PID means partial information decomposition: allocating information about
a specified target among source groups. These results do not validate
continuous shared exclusions or Williams–Beer I_min.

Categorical variables take declared labels; empirical joint probabilities
are counts divided by total count. Atoms are allocations from finite
inversion; cumulatives sum lower-or-equal atoms. Plus/minus are informative/
misinformative components; net is their signed difference. Nonnegative
components can yield negative net atoms. Nats use natural logarithms.
Finite arithmetic implies no sampling model.

## 1. Sharp entropy error for collapsing inputs

Fix a finite joint source/target law. Retain one source X; replace the
tuple C of all other sources by a constant while preserving the entire
joint law of X and the target. Do not filter rows where C is constant.

Let H(C) be entropy of C: mean negative natural logarithm of its label
probabilities, in nats. The largest absolute change over **all averaged
atoms and cumulatives, plus/minus/net, equals H(C)**. Three sources give
108 expressions. Net-only changes need not attain that maximum; local
pointwise values are not covered.

Positivity, entropy partitions and event inclusion bound changes;
a nuisance-equality cumulative attains H(C). See [every step and
worked controls](SXPID_CONSTANT_CHANNEL_ENTROPY_BOUND_2026_10_12.md).

Use: screen allocation changes before collapsing nearly constant inputs.
No predictive-utility guarantee. Hand-derived/source-reviewed;
formal and implementation verification open.

## 2. Cancellation-free numerical evaluation

For an exact empirical law, multiply positive rational arguments with
their integer cell multiplicities before taking logarithms. For one atom, let P and Q be the positive integer numerator/denominator,
N the positive total row count and A the signed average, in nats:

$$
A=\ln(P/Q)/N.
$$

Here ln is the natural logarithm. Comparing P and Q decides sign or exact
zero. For a nonzero value, range reduction expresses the magnitude as a
sum of nonnegative logarithms; a finite positive series bounds its tail.

With five terms and exact rational endpoints, midpoint relative error is
at most **1/1,154,736**, below one part per million. Relative error means
absolute midpoint error divided by |A|; exact zero has a separate branch.
[The full tail proof and worked examples](CANCELLATION_FREE_SIGNED_AVERAGE_2026_10_11.md) include a one-term failure.

Use: audit small signed averages harmed by subtracting rounded components.
Conditional proposal; not formally accepted, implemented or timed.
Flags stay unchanged. At N<=31,000, direct products have a 3,720,001-bit
ceiling each; fixed terms do not imply fixed cost. Rounding/Rust proof and
comparison with existing enclosing logarithms remain open.

The three-source carrier has 18 valid antichains: nonempty families of
nonempty source groups with no group containing another. Binary masks
encode source **positions**, not binary observed data.

All 19 original statements compile/pass the unchanged Lean judge:
coverage, reversible encoding, order/logical correspondence and all 324
incidence entries. Both compiled modules pass fresh shipped Lean replay.
No kernel, judge, limits or accepted-axiom policy changed.

## 5. Real-data results and failures

The [two-dataset numerical screen](EMPIRICAL_COMPONENT_BOUND_SCREEN_2026_10_11.md) uses fourteen overlapping
empirical laws, not independent replications. Sources are training-fitted
four-label light, carbon dioxide and temperature; the declared target is
occupancy or occupant count.

All 3,924 local and 504 averaged reference intervals were contained.
The cheap bound met four-part-per-million precision for 313 of 2,085
positive locals, but **none of 184 positive averages**. Enclosures establish no
population coverage or prediction gains.

[Exact integer signs](EXACT_EMPIRICAL_ATOM_SIGNS_2026_10_11.md) confirmed
252 averaged atoms: 89 positive, 15 negative, 148 exact zero.
The 55 cheap-bracket-unresolved signs were already known from references;
they are confirmations, not newly discovered signs.

The [recorded motion-collapse negative](CONSTANT_CHANNEL_OCCUPANCY_SCREEN_2026_10_12.md) retains 10,129 UCI864
motion/target rows: 1,198 constant-tuple departures and 727 occupied rows
without motion. Native counts plus analytic inequalities imply full-vector
collapse error between one-third and 0.53 nats, and a net-atom change above
0.01 nat for every compatible finite retained source. This is not executed
full PID, population confidence or a prediction-loss comparison.

Neural/imaging, embodied and world-model uses need justified variables,
observed targets, dependence-aware uncertainty and task-loss comparators.
No measured win; generated futures are not ground truth.
