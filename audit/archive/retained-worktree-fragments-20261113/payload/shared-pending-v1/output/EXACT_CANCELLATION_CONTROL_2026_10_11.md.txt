# Exact cancellation does not mean absent information

Sepehr Mahmoudian · 11 October 2026

## Result, question and evidence

Can an averaged categorical shared-exclusions atom be exactly zero while
every occupied cell has a nonzero signed contribution? Yes, for the complete
finite law below. Its full source tuple still determines the binary target.

One bounded exact-count execution now checks four fixed tables and 144 family
tables in normal and optimized modes. The general-family, minimum/gap, MI and
forecast arguments remain hand/source-reviewed, not kernel or human verified.
No priority or program closure is claimed. Separate exposed critiques check
the family; no old fixture, alphabet, precision flag or kernel changed.

Zero averaged shared information need not mean zero local terms or no
prediction. This also tests whether a future exact-sign verifier recognizes
cancellation across cells, rather than only all-local-zero cases.

## Source, glossary and input contract

Categorical Makkeh–Gutknecht–Wibral (MGW) shared exclusions is defined in
*Introducing a differentiable measure of pointwise shared information*,
[arXiv:2002.03356v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2002.03356v5),
[Phys. Rev. E 103, 032149](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.103.032149),
Equations (1)–(3), (13)–(15) and (17). The
[repository source map](../claims/SX-CERTIFIED-AVERAGED-PID3-001/source-correspondence-v4.md)
retains its correspondence limits. This construction/family is project-derived,
not attributed to that paper.

A probability mass function assigns normalized mass to categorical tuples;
support means positive-mass tuples. An empirical law divides row counts by
their total. An antichain contains source subsets, none contained in another.
The bottom comprises three singleton subsets. Its atom equals its cumulative
shared information because no ordinary node is lower. “Local” means at one
supported tuple; “averaged” means complete-joint expectation. A nat is the
unit for the natural logarithm, written \(\ln\). Logarithmic loss is minus
the logarithm of a forecast's probability for the actual target.
Mutual information (MI), defined below, is not this shared-exclusions atom.

The three sources \(S_1,S_2,S_3\) have labels in \(\{0,1,2,3,4\}\).
The target \(T\) has labels in \(\{0,1\}\). Labels have no physical units.
Let \(c_z\) be the positive integer count of an occupied complete tuple \(z\);
\(N\) is the total count. Each tuple's probability \(p(z)\) is \(c_z/N\).
All other tuples of the 250-cell Cartesian alphabet have probability zero.
No independence, continuous support, quantization, stationarity, causality or
population inference is assumed. This is one law and one PID definition.

## Complete 24-row law and event reconstruction

Row names identify occupied cells, not additional variables.

| Row | \(S_1\) | \(S_2\) | \(S_3\) | \(T\) | \(c_z\) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| n00 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 3 |
| n01 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 3 |
| n10 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 3 |
| n11 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 3 |
| a0 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 0 | 2 |
| a1 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 0 | 2 |
| a2 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 0 | 2 |
| b0 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 2 |
| b1 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| b2 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 2 |

Ten cells have \(N=4\cdot3+6\cdot2=24\) rows.
Each target has count twelve. No source globally duplicates another;
\(S_3=S_2\) holds only in n rows.

Fix a supported tuple \(z=(s_1,s_2,s_3,t)\). Its bottom-node event \(E_z\)
means \(S_1=s_1\) OR \(S_2=s_2\) OR \(S_3=s_3\). Define \(U_z\) as its
complete-row count, \(V_z\) as its count jointly with \(T=t\), and \(C_t\)
as that target's count. The anchor itself ensures all three counts are positive.

Every event's occupied members follow. Disjoint source labels prevent
cross-block matches.

| Anchor | Event members | \(U_z\) | \(V_z\) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| n00 | n00,n01,n10 | 9 | 3 |
| n01 | n00,n01,n11 | 9 | 3 |
| n10 | n00,n10,n11 | 9 | 3 |
| n11 | n01,n10,n11 | 9 | 3 |
| a0 | a0,a1,a2,b0 | 8 | 6 |
| a1 | a0,a1,a2,b1 | 8 | 6 |
| a2 | a0,a1,a2,b2 | 8 | 6 |
| b0 | b0,b1,b2,a0 | 8 | 6 |
| b1 | b0,b1,b2,a1 | 8 | 6 |
| b2 | b0,b1,b2,a2 | 8 | 6 |

For example, a0 matches a1 through the first source, a2 through the second,
and b0 through the third, as well as itself. Its event has four count-two
cells, three with its target: \(U_{\mathrm{a0}}=8,V_{\mathrm{a0}}=6\).

The informative argument \(R_z^+=N/U_z\) and misinformative argument
\(R_z^-=C_t/V_z\) are both positive. Their superscripts identify components,
not arithmetic signs. Define the net ratio \(q_z=R_z^+/R_z^-\). Then

\[
q_z=\frac{N V_z}{U_z C_t}
=\frac{p(E_z\mid T=t)}{p(E_z)},\qquad
\text{local signed atom}=\ln q_z.
\]

For n rows, \(R_z^+=24/9=8/3,R_z^-=12/3=4,q_z=2/3\).
For a/b rows, \(R_z^+=24/8=3,R_z^-=12/6=2,q_z=3/2\).
Thus every local net value is nonzero. Each separate component is positive.

Let \(A\) denote the averaged signed bottom atom in nats. All steps are

\[
\begin{aligned}
A
&=\sum_z \frac{c_z}{24}\ln q_z\\
&=\frac{4\cdot3}{24}\ln(2/3)
 +\frac{6\cdot2}{24}\ln(3/2)\\
&=\tfrac12\ln(2/3)+\tfrac12\ln(3/2)\\
&=\tfrac12\ln\big((2/3)(3/2)\big)=\tfrac12\ln1=0.
\end{aligned}
\]

Equivalently, the exact count product is
\((2/3)^{12}(3/2)^{12}=1\). No tolerance or floating-point logarithm is needed.
The informative and misinformative averages are separately
\(\tfrac12\ln(8/3)+\tfrac12\ln3=\tfrac12\ln8\) and
\(\tfrac12\ln4+\tfrac12\ln2=\tfrac12\ln8\).

## A solved restricted family, not global minimality

Keep the ten occupied tuples, but assign count \(a\) to every n row and count
\(b\) to every a/b row, where \(a,b\) are positive integers.
Now \(N=4a+6b\) and \(C_t=2a+3b=N/2\).
For n rows \(U_z=3a,V_z=a\); for a/b rows \(U_z=4b,V_z=3b\).
Substitution gives the SAME ratios \(2/3\) and \(3/2\), respectively.

Therefore, since \(\ln(2/3)=-\ln(3/2)\),

\[
A=\frac{6b-4a}{4a+6b}\ln(3/2)
=\frac{3b-2a}{2a+3b}\ln(3/2).
\]

The denominator and \(\ln(3/2)\) are positive. The atom's sign is precisely
the sign of integer \(3b-2a\). It is zero exactly when \(3b=2a\).
That equality makes \(a\) divisible by three: write \(a=3k\), where \(k\)
is a positive integer. Substitution gives \(b=2k,N=24k\).
Thus 24 is minimal ONLY in this block-constant family, not across all laws.

If \(A\ne0\), the nonzero integer numerator has absolute value at least one,
so

\[
|A|\ \ge\ \frac{\ln(3/2)}{2a+3b}
=\frac{2\ln(3/2)}{N}.
\]

Equality holds for \(a=b=1\), with \(N=10,A=\ln(3/2)/5\), and for
\(a=2,b=1\), with \(N=14,A=-\ln(3/2)/7\).
This bound is family-specific; arbitrary empirical atoms need not have it.
Increasing counts can make nonzero values arbitrarily small:
\(a=3k+1,b=2k+1\) gives numerator one and denominator \(12k+5\).
There is no count-independent positive gap. A relative error at exact zero
is undefined; a zero decision and a nonzero-magnitude bound are separate tasks.

## Prediction and MI are different questions

Let \(S=(S_1,S_2,S_3)\) be the complete source tuple. Its Shannon MI with \(T\)
is defined here by
\(I(S;T)=\sum_z p(z)\ln[p(T=t\mid S=s)/p(T=t)]\), with \(s=(s_1,s_2,s_3)\).
In the 24-row law, every source tuple has one target, so the conditional target
probability in that ratio is one. The target prior is one half. Every term
has log ratio \(\ln2\), and the weights sum to one: \(I(S;T)=\ln2>0\).

A full-tuple finite-law forecast chooses that unique target and has zero
classification error and zero logarithmic loss. A constant forecast has
error one half. A prior probability forecast assigns one half to each target
and has loss \(\ln2\). These are law calculations, not field benchmarks.

For source tuple \(s\), define a different forecast
\(g_s(u)=p(T=u\mid E_s)\), where \(u\) is either target label and \(E_s\) is its
row-specific OR event. It assigns the actual target probability \(3/9=1/3\)
in n rows and \(6/8=3/4\) in a/b rows. Hence its mean log loss is
\(\tfrac12\ln3+\tfrac12\ln(4/3)=\ln2\), exactly the prior's loss.
Its most likely label is wrong on every n row and right on every a/b row;
its error is one half. The shared-exclusions average is thus not the MI of
one fixed event channel. The event is reconstructed for each source tuple.

For future neural, imaging or physical-intelligence audits, retain local signs
and task comparators when an average is zero. This establishes no data benefit
or harmful-source identification and does not justify source removal.

## Preserved alternative and selection accounting

Four complete handcrafted laws were explored: three worker layouts and one
root law. Two primary notes were frozen before their authors saw each other's
law. Other layouts were retrospectively recorded, not contemporaneously frozen.
Selection was exploratory, not preregistered or empirically confirmatory.
The selected law has fewer rows/cells, a binary target, no global source copy
and an OR-event edge unique to each coordinate. Shared model, host, policy and
source prevent claims of full independence.

Both unselected worker layouts retain the four n cells/count3, and have six
positive cells/count2. Layout C1 uses \((2,i,i;0),(3,i,i;1)\), for
\(i\in\{2,3,4\}\): source triple then target. It has global \(S_3=S_2\).
C2 uses \((2,2,2;0),(2,3,2;0),(2,4,4;0),(3,2,3;1),(3,3,3;1),(3,4,4;1)\).
It has no global copy, but its third OR branch adds no event edge. Both retain
the same \(2/3,3/2\) ratios and zero average. They are valid, not failed laws.

The root's alternative has \(N=60\), fourteen occupied cells, source labels
0–4 and target labels 0–2, with \(S_3=S_1\). Its COMPLETE grouped law below
writes target:count pairs; omitted targets have zero count.

| Source tuple | Target:count |
|---|---|
| 000 | 1:4, 2:4 |
| 111 | 0:4, 2:4 |
| 222 | 0:4, 1:4 |
| 333 | 0:6, 2:3 |
| 343 | 1:6, 2:3 |
| 434 | 1:6, 2:3 |
| 444 | 0:6, 2:3 |

Each target has count twenty. The first three source tuples produce local
ratio \(3/2\) on 24 rows. The last four give \(2/3\) on 24 rows and one on
twelve target-two rows. Thus its bottom average is also zero, while
\(I(S;T)=(2/5)\ln3>0\); its full-source optimal prediction rate is \(3/5\),
versus \(1/3\) for a constant prediction. MI and prediction remain hand-derived;
the exact bottom counts/product also passed the bounded execution below.
This larger duplicate-source control is not an extra dataset.

## Bounded exact execution

The [complete outputs and run facts](data/exact-cancellation-control-20261011/STATUS.md)
retain the one fixed audit. Each mode checked C1, C2, C3, root60 and every
ordered \((a,b)\) in \(\{1,\ldots,12\}^2\): 148 evaluations and 147 distinct
count tables, not independent samples or distinct probability laws.
All exact products/signs matched: 44 negative, 8 zero and 96 positive.
The eight zeros include four fixed tables and four family pairs.
Thirty-five negative controls were refused for their assigned reason; four
positive controls passed. An independent construction of integer powers of
two and three checked every ordered scientific record without importing the
event loops. Both modes agreed after removing only their optimization field.

Native exits were zero, output was complete and stderr empty. Scientific
invocations took 54,992,584 and 55,584,625 recorded nanoseconds, within the
unchanged 15-second deadline; selected pins were unchanged. These are finite
run facts, not a speedup, generic caller qualification, formal proof, statistical
test or task benchmark. The run binds the preserved pre-execution report;
this editorial update occurred after closing pin checks. Its private custody
is not a portable public replay bundle. No future rerun inherits acceptance.

verifier above checks only bottom counts/products/signs, not all Rust atoms.

five labels per source, two targets and ten occupied joint cells.

comparisons plus integer additions for that direct scheme. Captured batch

inference, resampling, preprocessing, hard limit, cancellation guarantee,
