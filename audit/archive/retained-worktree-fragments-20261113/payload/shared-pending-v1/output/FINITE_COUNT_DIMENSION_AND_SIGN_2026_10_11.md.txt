# Finite-count precision: dimension, sign and explicit limits

Sepehr Mahmoudian · 11 October 2026

## Results and status

Exact categorical counts make several numerical questions tractable. The
positive-component guarantee extends to four sources. A nonzero local
three-source signed atom also has a weaker, useful relative guarantee.
Neither result silently transfers to signed averages or continuous data.

| Question | Hand-derived answer | Remaining obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Smallest positive local component, fixed pool count | Sharp formulas for one, two and three maximal excluded cells | New formal admission/refinement |
| Nonzero three-source local signed atom | Reference-midpoint relative error below \(1/128\); interval-based ceiling below \(1/126\) | Separate diagnostic implementation |
| Four-source positive component or positive average | Same 64-term/128-bit reference ceiling below \(2^{-44}\) | Four-source reference implementation/checks |
| Five sources with the same fixed 128-bit grid | A realizable positive example has midpoint error greater than 100% | Outside current helper; no executed failure |
| Exact signed average | Integer products decide sign/zero without logarithms | Prospective executable packet remains unrun |
| Prescribed positive-component precision, finite count cap | A declared adaptive grid/series choice suffices; 150 bits suffice for the stated five-source case | New implementation, resource admission and formal verification |

These are hand proofs with explicit analytic dependencies, not new accepted
kernel results, human verification, scientific-priority claims or deployment
evidence. The dimensional/sign arguments received independent-first council
review. The adaptive extension also received its distinct independent-first
derivation and exposed full source critique; root inspected both. It remains
unimplemented and unqualified. No original program closes here.

The defining measure is Makkeh–Gutknecht–Wibral (MGW) categorical shared
exclusions, not I_min or Ehrlich's continuous functional. The
[defining paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.103.032149) and
[versioned source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.03356v5) define the measure.
The [component derivation](POSITIVE_CATEGORICAL_COMPONENT_BOUNDS_2026_10_11.md)
supplies the full categorical inverse/integral correspondence; the
[three-source precision report](FINITE_COUNT_REFERENCE_PRECISION_2026_10_11.md)
supplies the exact series/grid proof. Bounds here are repository derivations,
not claims that those papers defined this numerical certificate.

## 1. Input, output and symbols

There are \(n\) categorical sources \(X_1,\ldots,X_n\) and a categorical
target \(T\), each with a finite alphabet. Let \(I=\{1,\ldots,n\}\) be the
source-position set. A finite count law contains \(N\) rows. Every occupied
joint anchor \(s=(x_1,\ldots,x_n,t)\) has positive integer count \(c_s\),
and \(\sum_s c_s=N\). Lowercase entries are fixed labels, not variables.
The declared cap is the integer \(M=31000\), with \(1\le N\le M\), except
where the adaptive section explicitly permits other integer \(M\ge2\).

For another source row \(y\), its agreement pattern is
\(B(y)=\{i\in I:y_i=x_i\}\). An ordinary antichain \(\alpha\) is a
nonempty family of nonempty subsets of \(I\), with no subset containing
another. Event \(E_\alpha\) accepts a row when all positions of at least
one branch agree: AND inside a branch, OR between branches.
Excluded agreement patterns form a downward-closed family. Its maximal
members \(B_1,\ldots,B_k\) have no larger excluded member; \(k\ge1\).
They are exact equality cells, not unions of smaller cells.

For one nonnegative component choose law \(Q\): source marginal for the
informative side, or source law conditional on the anchor target \(T=t\)
for the misinformative side. Its count denominator is \(D=N\) or
\(D=C_t\), respectively, where \(C_t\) counts rows with target label \(t\).
Write \(u=Q(E_\alpha)>0\), \(a_i=Q(B(y)=B_i)\). All these masses are
nonnegative multiples of \(1/D\), \(u\ge1/D\), and

\[
u+\sum_{i=1}^k a_i\le1,\qquad 1\le D\le N\le M.
\]

The output \(p\) is this component in nats, using natural logarithms.
Superscripts \(+,-\) identify informative/misinformative components;
their signed difference is \(\sigma=p^+-p^-\). Bars denote exact within-law
averaging with weights \(c_s/N\), not averaging datasets.

No source independence, Gaussian/continuous/angular support, independent
and identically distributed rows, stationarity or confidence-coverage
assumption is needed for count arithmetic. That does not establish
population estimation, causal meaning or statistical calibration.
Quantization, source choice, target definition and row alignment must be
declared separately; changing any of them changes the empirical object.

## 2. Analytic dependency and sharp local minima

The full categorical inverse gives the positive box integral

\[
p_k(u;a_1,\ldots,a_k)
=(k-1)!\int_0^{a_1}\cdots\int_0^{a_k}
 (u+\eta_1+\cdots+\eta_k)^{-k}\,d\eta_k\cdots d\eta_1
=\ln R.
\]

The integration coordinates \(\eta_i\) are mass coordinates, not measured
data; \(!\) is factorial, with \(0!=1\). Subscript \(k\) identifies cover
order, not source index. The exact positive rational argument is

\[
R=
\frac{\prod_{J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\},\ |J|\text{ odd}}
 (u+\sum_{i\in J}a_i)}
{\prod_{J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\},\ |J|\text{ even}}
 (u+\sum_{i\in J}a_i)}.
\]

Here \(J\) is an index subset and \(|J|\) its size; the even product
includes the empty subset, contributing \(u\). Equal odd/even factor
counts cancel the common count denominator. If any \(a_i=0\), then
\(p=0\) exactly; otherwise it is positive. Ordinary bottom's extra
term is the virtual certain event, whose cumulative value is zero.
It is not an ordinary additional atom or the false empty antichain.

Fix \(D,k\) and a positive component. Every \(a_i\ge1/D\), so
\(u\le(D-k)/D\). Increasing a rectangle width increases the integral;
decreasing \(u\) increases its positive integrand. Consequently the
minimum is at \(a_i=1/D\) and \(u=(D-k)/D\). It exists only for
\(D\ge k+1\); if \(D\le k\), there is no positive case. Substitution gives

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
k & \text{sharp local minimum}\\ \hline
1&\ln\dfrac{D}{D-1}\\[3pt]
2&\ln\dfrac{(D-1)^2}{D(D-2)}\\[3pt]
3&\ln\dfrac{D(D-2)^3}{(D-3)(D-1)^3}.
\end{array}
\]

Sharpness is realizable, not merely a relaxed mass optimization. Use binary
sources, an all-zero anchor with \(D-k\) full-agreement rows, one row for
each maximal excluded pattern, and a constant target. Agreeing positions
are zero; other positions one. Three-source nodes for \(k=1,2,3\) are
respectively \(\{\{1\},\{2\},\{3\}\}\),
\(\{\{1,2\},\{1,3\}\}\), and \(\{\{1,2,3\}\}\).
For the second node the excluded maxima are \(\{1\},\{2,3\}\); for the
third they are the three pairs. Constant target makes the two components
equal: their signed atom is zero despite these positive components.

An easier, nonsharp bound suffices for numerical precision. Throughout
the box \(u+\sum_i\eta_i\le1\), so

\[
p>0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p\ge\frac{(k-1)!}{D^k}.
\]

## 3. Range and reference width do not depend on three sources

Integrate the final coordinate for \(k\ge2\):

\[
p_k(u;a_1,\ldots,a_k)
=p_{k-1}(u;a_1,\ldots,a_{k-1})
-p_{k-1}(u+a_k;a_1,\ldots,a_{k-1}).
\]

Both right components are nonnegative. Iterating the upper inequality yields
\(p_k\le p_1=\ln(1+a_1/u)\le\ln D\). Hence \(1\le R\le D\le M\).
Write \(R=2^e v\), with integer exponent \(e\ge0\) and rational
\(1\le v<2\). Here \(v\) is a reduced logarithm argument, not variance
or velocity. Since \(M<2^{15}\), \(e\le14\).

For the present 64-term reference, the combined raw logarithm interval
width is at most \(15/(172\,3^{128})\). Set grid step \(h=2^{-128}\).
That width is below \(h\). Integer outward floor/ceiling gives endpoints
\(L,U\) with \(L\le p\le U\) and \(U-L\le2h\); its midpoint has
absolute error at most \(h\). Structural zeros give exactly \([0,0]\).
These are exact rational operations, not a binary64 directed-rounding claim.
The [preceding report](FINITE_COUNT_REFERENCE_PRECISION_2026_10_11.md#step-3-the-64-term-raw-interval-is-narrower-than-one-grid-step)
includes every series-tail and integer-grid step.

If every positive local component is at least \(\delta>2h\), its
computable relative ceiling is at most \(h/(\delta-2h)\). For a positive
average let \(W\) be the sum of weights of positive local components.
Average lower endpoint is at least \(W(\delta-2h)\), and width at most
\(2hW\). Thus \(W>0\) cancels, giving the same ceiling. This requires
nonnegative exact weights and exact zero references, not signed cancellation.

## 4. Nonzero three-source local signed atoms

Each count factor in \(R\) lies between one and its pool denominator.
For \(k\le3\), each odd/even product has at most four factors.
Write \(R^+=A^+/F^+\), \(R^-=A^-/F^-\), with positive integer
numerators and denominators bounded by \(M^4\). The letters \(A,F\)
here name integers, not events or probability laws. Cross-multiplication gives

\[
\sigma=\ln(C/F),\quad C=A^+F^-,\quad F=F^+A^-,
\qquad 1\le C,F\le H=M^8.
\]

If \(C=F\), the signed atom is exactly zero. Otherwise integrate
\(1/x\) between the two distinct positive integers:

\[
|\sigma|\ge\frac{|C-F|}{\max(C,F)}\ge\frac1H.
\]

The conditional and marginal counts are coupled; the bound respects that
coupling but is not claimed sharp. Cross-subtract reference intervals to
get \([L^+-U^-,U^+-L^-]\), width \(w\le4h\), midpoint error at most
\(2h\). Because \(M<2^{15}\), \(H<2^{120}\), and therefore

\[
\frac{|\text{midpoint}-\sigma|}{|\sigma|}
\le2hH<\frac1{128}<1\%\quad(\sigma\ne0).
\]

Also \(4h<1/H\). A valid local reference interval containing zero must
represent an exact zero: otherwise it contains both zero and a value at
least \(1/H\) away, contradicting its width. A nonzero interval therefore
has the correct strict sign. Midpoint zero alone is not this argument.
Exact integer comparison is a simpler zero test.

For a strict interval let \(d\) be its endpoint closest to zero in absolute
value. Then \(d\ge1/H-4h\), so the interval-based computable ceiling is

\[
\frac{w}{2d}\le\frac{2hH}{1-4hH}<\frac1{126}<1\%.
\]

This denominator is an interval endpoint, not the true value used in the
\(1/128\) bound. When \(k\le2\), use \(H=M^4<2^{60}\) instead:
the endpoint-based ceiling is below \(2^{-66}\), hence below four ppm.
For \(k=3\), this proof establishes 1%, not uniform four-ppm signed
accuracy. No actual four-ppm failure is claimed.

The existing helper's signed labels are from its **Taylor** intervals,
not the references. These new hand conclusions do not alter those labels,
midpoints, thresholds or screen results; reference sign/relative diagnostics
are not currently implemented by that helper.

## 5. Four sources: positive precision and a signed boundary

Maximal excluded patterns form an antichain of subsets of four positions.
Every permutation gives a nested chain of subsets. A size-\(j\) pattern
lies in \(j!(4-j)!\) of the \(4!=24\) chains, while an antichain meets
each chain at most once. Dividing the count by 24 gives

\[
\sum_{i=1}^k\frac1{\binom4{|B_i|}}\le1.
\]

The binomial coefficient counts subsets of the stated size. Each is at
most six, so \(k\le6\). This is the classical chain-count argument,
not new mathematics: [Lubell's original proof, 1966](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0021-9800(66)80035-2),
also available as a [one-page scan](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/akherim/lubell1966.pdf).
The four-triple node, whose branches are all four subsets of size three,
attains the bound: its excluded maxima are the six pairs.

For \(k\le6\), successive \((k-1)!/M^k\) decrease because their
ratio is \(k/M<1\). Set \(\delta_4=120/M^6\), valid for every positive
component. Since \(M<2^{15}\), \(\delta_4>15\,2^{-87}\). The unchanged
64-term/128-bit mathematical reference therefore has ceiling

\[
\frac{h}{\delta_4-2h}
=\frac{M^6}{120\,2^{128}-2M^6}
<\frac1{15\,2^{41}-2}<2^{-44}<\frac1{250000}.
\]

Exact positive averaging preserves this bound by Section 3. This is an
extension of the analytic argument, not execution of a four-source reference:
the current research helper fixes three sources/eighteen nodes and refuses
four sources. The general identity does not supply its absent code refinement.

Signed four-source cross-products can have height \(M^{64}\); the preceding
width/gap proof no longer establishes a useful uniform 128-bit signed
guarantee. This is a limitation of that proof, not an observed failure.
A realizable sufficient case has the four-triple node, \(M-6\) full-agreement
rows and one at each pair, with the anchor target assigned to all rows
except one pair. The conditional component is exactly zero, so the positive
net equals the marginal component and inherits its guarantee. If instead
the target is constant, both positive components agree and the net is zero.

## 6. Five-source obstruction under the same count cap

Take five binary sources, all ten triple branches, one row at each of the
ten pair patterns and \(D-10\) full-agreement rows, with \(D=M=31000\).
Use a constant target. The anchor is supported, \(k=10\), and
\(u=(D-10)/D\), \(a_i=1/D\). The positive integral gives

\[
0<p\le\frac{9!}{(D-10)^{10}}<\frac h4.
\]

The last inequality is exact: \(D-10>15\,2^{11}\), and
\(9!\,2^{20}=380507258880<576650390625=15^{10}\).
The combined 64-term tail is also below \(h/4\):
\(3^{128}>2^{192}\), and \(15/172<1/8\) give raw width
below \(2^{-195}<h/4\). Its raw lower is positive and its upper below
\(h\). Hypothetical outward rounding to the fixed 128-bit grid gives
\([0,h]\). The midpoint \(h/2\) has relative error
\(h/(2p)-1>1\), greater than 100%.

This is a realizable categorical law, outside the implemented three-source
helper and current two-to-four-source Rust API. It is not an in-contract
failure or executed benchmark. Constant target again makes its signed net
zero; the obstruction concerns a positive component, not prediction gain.

## 7. Adaptive positive precision — hand/source reviewed, unimplemented

This section defines a **different prospective reference configuration**.
It does not rewrite the fixed-grid counterexample, change old flags or
waive their criteria. Let \(n\ge1\) be finite, \(M\ge2\) an integer
row cap and \(\varepsilon>0\) a desired rational relative ceiling.
The same chain count gives
\(K=\binom n{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\), a bound on excluded maxima.
Floor means rounding down. A positive component has at least one count
in each maximal cell and in the event, so \(k+1\le D\le M\).
Define \(\kappa=\min(K,M-1)\), and

\[
\delta=\frac{(\kappa-1)!}{M^\kappa}>0.
\]

Monotonicity of the elementary bounds for \(k<M\) makes \(\delta\)
a uniform positive gap. Choose integer grid bits \(B\ge1\) such that

\[
2^B\delta\ge\frac{1+2\varepsilon}{\varepsilon}.
\]

Now grid step is \(h_B=2^{-B}\). Rearrangement gives
\(h_B/(\delta-2h_B)\le\varepsilon\), with positive denominator.
Choose nonnegative integers \(E,L\) with \(M<2^{E+1}\) and
\(2^L\ge E+1\). The range exponent is at most \(E\).
Choose series term count \(q\ge1\) with \(3q\ge B+L\).
For a reduced argument \(v\in[1,2]\), put \(z=(v-1)/(v+1)\le1/3\).
Keeping indices \(0,\ldots,q-1\) bounds its omitted tail by

\[
\tau_q\le\frac{2z^{2q+1}}{(2q+1)(1-z^2)}
\le\frac{3}{4(2q+1)3^{2q}}<2^{-3q}.
\]

The last inequality uses \(3^2>2^3\). Combined raw width is below
\((E+1)2^{-3q}\le2^{-B}\). The exact grid proof therefore gives width
at most \(2h_B\) and relative ceiling at most \(\varepsilon\), for
every positive component and positive exact average. Structural zeros
remain exact zeros. Negative weights or approximate mass conversion do not
inherit this argument; signed-average relative precision is not established.

For five sources and \(M=31000\), \(\kappa=10\). Since
\(9!>2^{18}\) and \(M<2^{15}\), \(\delta>2^{-132}\).
A separately declared 151-bit grid, still keeping 64 terms, has raw width
below \(2^{-195}<2^{-151}\) as above. Its ceiling is therefore

\[
\frac{2^{-151}}{\delta-2\,2^{-151}}
<\frac1{2^{19}-2}=\frac1{524286}<\frac1{250000}.
\]

The independent-first derivation gives a smaller sufficient choice, without
claiming optimality. Since \(M^{10}<2^{150}\), the exact gap obeys
\(\delta=9!/M^{10}>9!\,2^{-150}\). With a separately declared 150-bit
grid and the same 64 terms, the raw width is still below the grid step;
the relative ceiling is

\[
\frac{2^{-150}}{\delta-2\,2^{-150}}
<\frac1{9!-2}=\frac1{362878}<\frac1{250000}.
\]

The original 151-bit proposal remains a valid, nonminimal choice. Neither
alternative changes the old 128-bit obstruction. Root read the entire fresh
adaptive critique; it agrees with the general rule and these sufficient
choices. A new source contract, resource admission, numerical refinement
and formal verification are required before accepting an executable
certificate. No implementation or new run exists.

## 8. Signed averages: exact sign is a different question

For each occupied anchor write its exact local ratio as \(C_s/F_s\),
positive integers. With marginal count weights on both components,

\[
\bar\sigma=\frac1N\ln(P/Q),\qquad
P=\prod_s C_s^{c_s},\quad Q=\prod_s F_s^{c_s}.
\]

Here \(P,Q\) are integer products, not probability laws. Comparing them
decides positive, negative or exact zero, without floating logarithms or
a separation threshold. For three sources \(P,Q\le M^{8N}\), giving
only the weak nonzero gap \(1/(NM^{8N})\). It does not transfer the useful
local 128-bit relative guarantee to averages. No actual capped failure or
sharpness claim follows from that conservative gap.

The old screen retains 55 Taylor sign-unresolved averages. A new exact
integer diagnostic may accompany them, but must not relabel their intervals
or precision outcomes. Its source preparation is separate and unrun.
Exact sign alone gives no magnitude, population uncertainty or task benefit.

## 9. Rust implementation and computational cost

The stable categorical Rust API supports two through four sources, with
4, 18 and 166 ordinary nodes. It implements empirical MGW atoms, not these
rational interval certificates. The existing helper evaluates three sources;
no new Rust interval implementation/refinement or higher-source benchmark
is claimed. See the [implementation baseline](../RUST_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_COST.md).

Given correct masses, an alternating reference needs \(2^k\) count factors
before logarithm work. Adaptive \(q,B\), rational numerator/denominator
sizes and greatest-common-divisor work have nonconstant bit costs. The
maximum antichain order grows combinatorially with \(n\); the positive-count
cap \(\kappa\) does not bound the cost of enumerating the entire carrier.
No universal practical runtime follows from finite mathematical existence.

Three-source local cross-products are below \(2^{120}\), so a future u128
comparison is plausible, not verified. Three-source average products have
at most \(120N+1\le3720001\) bits each. Streaming one node limits retained
payload, not powering/gcd workspace, total resident memory or deadline.
Four-source averages have a much larger conservative bit bound. Missing,
malformed, canceled, out-of-budget or timed-out computations must abstain,
not return zero or a fabricated sign. No new timing or memory result exists.

## 10. Uses, rejected transfers and closure

The [two-UCI-dataset occupancy screen](EMPIRICAL_COMPONENT_BOUND_SCREEN_2026_10_11.md)
addresses a real room-occupancy estimation problem and supplies count laws
inside the cap. Its numerical/reference evidence is not measured classifier
gain. The proposed reference/sign certificates could separate numerical
ambiguity from an exact empirical negative or zero before testing a sensor
retention decision against direct prediction/loss baselines.

Four categorical sources may include a fourth independently measured stream;
that is not permission to regard a generated forecast as a new observation.
For embodied/world-model studies, keep declared context, forecasts and actual
future outcomes distinct as in the [decision report](WORLD_MODEL_PID_DECISION_2026_10_11.md).
Neural event labels or brain-image categories also require justified encoding,
subjects/episodes and aligned outcomes. These are proposed mappings, not
executed imaging, neural or robot results. Continuous volumes, angular data
and hyperbolic embeddings require their own support/estimand contracts.

Rejected shortcuts: positive-component precision implies signed precision;
midpoint zero proves zero; fixed 128 bits covers every dimension; observed
cells identify population support; absolute rarity controls relative ratios;
count arithmetic supplies confidence coverage; higher precision repairs a
wrong target/encoding; formal tool changes or relaxed thresholds establish
proof. All original screen inputs, criteria, failures and labels remain.

Independent-first review reproduced local minima, local signed separation,
four-source positive bounds and dimensional/cancellation limits before fresh
proposal exchange. Later exposed review checked source correspondence and
the five-source construction. Root inspected the reasoning and full critique.
Shared model/context/host and prior formulas remain dependencies. Ten routes
and fifty named lenses are advisory, not independent human votes or proof.
The distinct independent-first adaptive critique is now read and agrees;
its subsequent source exposure is explicit, not a human/kernel acceptance.

New kernel admission, exact implementation refinement, statistical calibration,
matched task advantage and qualified matching PDF remain OPEN. SxPID3 A–E,
original nineteen formal targets, angular, hyperbolic and v1 remain OPEN.
A conditional numerical theorem is useful progress, not closure by renaming.
