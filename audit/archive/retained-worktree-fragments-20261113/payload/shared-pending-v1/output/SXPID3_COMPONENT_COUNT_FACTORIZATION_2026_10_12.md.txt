# Exact count formulas for all 18 categorical SxPID3 component atoms

12 October 2026. Source/HAND mathematical note.

All 18 local informative or misinformative atoms have exact positive-rational
logarithms: six one-pattern, nine two-pattern and two three-pattern difference
rows, plus the bottom node. Here `log1p(x)` means $\ln(1+x)$.
The three-pattern row has a single `log1p` expression
without box-integral approximation. Rounded evaluation still needs error analysis.

The functional is due to Makkeh, Gutknecht and Wibral,
[Physical Review E 103, 032149 (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.103.032149).
The events, components, averaging and inverse follow the retained
[MGW source correspondence](../claims/SX-CERTIFIED-AVERAGED-PID3-001/source-correspondence-v4.md).
This reformulation introduces no new PID or priority claim. The existing
[positive component derivation](POSITIVE_CATEGORICAL_COMPONENT_BOUNDS_2026_10_11.md)
already gives the ideal inverse, positive box integral and zero criterion.

## Count law and source events

Let $S_1,S_2,S_3$ be three ordered finite categorical sources and $T$ a finite
categorical target. A complete joint cell is $\omega=(s_1,s_2,s_3,t)$, with
integer count $c_\omega\ge0$. Set $N=\sum_\omega c_\omega>0$. The declared law
is the empirical law $c_\omega/N$, not an inferred population distribution.
Only anchors with $c_\omega>0$ are evaluated. No independence or sampling
assumption is needed for the count identities. Information units are nats.

Put $I=\{1,2,3\}$. A node $\alpha$ is a nonempty antichain of nonempty subsets
of $I$: no distinct branch contains another. Its event at anchor $\omega$ is

$$
E_\alpha(\omega)=
\bigcup_{A\in\alpha}\ \bigcap_{i\in A}\{S_i=s_i\}.
$$

Thus equalities are joined by AND inside a branch and OR across branches.
Define $U_\alpha(\omega)$ as the count of this source event,
$V_\alpha(\omega)$ as its count additionally restricted to $T=t$, and $N_t$
as the count of $T=t$. Anchor membership gives

$$
0<c_\omega\le V_\alpha\le U_\alpha\le N,
\qquad V_\alpha\le N_t\le N.
$$

The local cumulative components are
$f_\alpha^+(\omega)=\ln(N/U_\alpha)$ and
$f_\alpha^-(\omega)=\ln(N_t/V_\alpha)$, informative and misinformative
respectively. The signed cumulative is $f_\alpha^+-f_\alpha^-$.
Misinformative is a component name, not a negative sign.

For comparison cell $\eta=(s'_1,s'_2,s'_3,t')$, define its agreement pattern
$M(\eta;\omega)=\{i\in I:s'_i=s_i\}$. There are **eight** patterns, including
$\varnothing$ and $I$. Let $n_M(\omega)$ sum cell counts $c_\eta$ in pattern
$M$; $n_M^t(\omega)$ additionally restricts $t'=t$. Their sums over all eight
patterns are $N$ and $N_t$, respectively.

## Ideals and all 18 canonical keys

Let $\mathcal H$ be the six proper nonempty subsets of $I$, ordered by inclusion.
Write $1$ for $\{1\}$, $12$ for $\{1,2\}$, and similarly for the other pattern
labels. Define the exclusion ideal

$$
J_\alpha=\{M\in\mathcal H:\text{no }A\in\alpha\text{ is a subset of }M\}.
$$

An ideal includes each member's predecessors. The event excludes exactly
$\varnothing$ and $J_\alpha$; pattern $I$ is covered. Each ideal gives one node
whose branches are the minimal covered patterns. There are eight ideals with
no pair pattern, six with one pair, three with two pairs and one with three: 18.

The redundancy order is $\alpha\preceq\beta$ iff every branch of $\beta$
contains a branch of $\alpha$, equivalently $J_\alpha\subseteq J_\beta$.
The cumulative at a node sums atoms at lower-or-equal nodes.

Source masks `01`, `02`, `04` mean sources 1, 2, 3; other masks join selected bits.
Keys are sorted mask families; `+` separates branches. In the ideal columns,
`{1,2,3}` means three singleton patterns; the full agreement pattern is `123`.
$\operatorname{Max}(J)$ denotes the maximal elements of the ideal, and
$k=|\operatorname{Max}(J)|$.

## Local inverse and exact rational gaps

At one anchor define

$$
u=N-n_\varnothing-\sum_{M\in J}n_M,
\qquad
v=N_t-n_\varnothing^t-\sum_{M\in J}n_M^t.
$$

Both are positive event counts. For a nonempty ideal, order its maximal
patterns as $M_1,\ldots,M_k$. For the informative channel set $w=u$ and
$x_j=n_{M_j}$; for the misinformative channel set $w=v$ and $x_j=n_{M_j}^t$.
Here $w>0$ and every $x_j\ge0$ is an integer; $j=1,\ldots,k$.

Deleting a subset of maximal patterns leaves an ideal and adds exactly their
disjoint counts to the event. The inverse coefficient is $(-1)^{|D|}$ for
each deleted set $D\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\}$. Therefore the local component atom
$\pi_\alpha$ in the chosen channel is

$$
\pi_\alpha=
\sum_{D\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\}}(-1)^{|D|}
\ln\frac{L}{w+\sum_{j\in D}x_j},
$$

where $L=N$ for informative and $L=N_t$ for misinformative. Summing deletion
rows over subideals gives zero off-diagonal: the coefficient alternates over
subsets of a nonempty set of minimal missing elements. The diagonal is one.
This proves inversion on this ideal poset, not arbitrary event posets.

For $k\ge1$, the constant $\ln L$ cancels. Define the denominator $Q_k$ and
nonnegative numerator gap $G_k$ by

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_1&=w,&G_1&=x_1,\\
Q_2&=w(w+x_1+x_2),&G_2&=x_1x_2,\\
Q_3&=w(w+x_1+x_2)(w+x_1+x_3)(w+x_2+x_3),&
G_3&=x_1x_2x_3(2w+x_1+x_2+x_3).
\end{aligned}
$$

Then every nonbottom component atom is exactly

$$
\pi_\alpha=\ln\left(1+\frac{G_k}{Q_k}\right).
$$

For $k=1$ this is $\ln[(w+x_1)/w]$. For $k=2$ it is
$\ln[(w+x_1)(w+x_2)/(w(w+x_1+x_2))]$: the numerator minus denominator is
$x_1x_2$. For $k=3$ the alternating row gives numerator
$P_3=(w+x_1)(w+x_2)(w+x_3)(w+x_1+x_2+x_3)$ and denominator $Q_3$.

To check the three-pattern gap explicitly, put
$\sigma_1=x_1+x_2+x_3$, $\sigma_2=x_1x_2+x_1x_3+x_2x_3$, and
$\sigma_3=x_1x_2x_3$. Direct expansion gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_3&=w^4+2\sigma_1w^3+(\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2)w^2
 +(\sigma_1\sigma_2+\sigma_3)w+\sigma_1\sigma_3,\\
Q_3&=w^4+2\sigma_1w^3+(\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2)w^2
 +(\sigma_1\sigma_2-\sigma_3)w.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus $P_3-Q_3=\sigma_3(2w+\sigma_1)=G_3$. Since $Q_k>0$, every component
atom is nonnegative and, for a nonempty ideal, it vanishes exactly when at
least one maximal-pattern count in that channel is zero.

The bottom ideal is empty and its inverse row has only one term. Do not cancel
$\ln L$ at $k=0$. Its informative and misinformative atoms are respectively

$$
\pi_{\rm bottom}^+=\ln(N/u)=\ln\left(1+\frac{N-u}{u}\right),
\qquad
\pi_{\rm bottom}^-=\ln(N_t/v)=\ln\left(1+\frac{N_t-v}{v}\right).
$$

They vanish exactly when $u=N$ or $v=N_t$, respectively. The signed atom is
always $\pi_\alpha^+-\pi_\alpha^-$; positivity of the components does not
give its sign, and subtraction may cancel exactly.

## A realizable four row example

Take binary source alphabets, a constant target, and exactly this occupied law.
Every unlisted joint cell has count zero; total $N=N_t=4$.

| Sources $(S_1,S_2,S_3)$ | Target | Count | Pattern relative to anchor `000` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `000` | 0 | 1 | 123 |
| `001` | 0 | 1 | 12 |
| `010` | 0 | 1 | 13 |
| `100` | 0 | 1 | 23 |

At anchor `000`, key `07` fixes all three sources. Its ideal is all of
$\mathcal H$, so $u=v=w=1$ and the maximal-pattern increments are
$x_1=x_2=x_3=1$. Consequently

$$
Q_3=1\cdot3\cdot3\cdot3=27,\qquad
G_3=1\cdot1\cdot1\cdot(2+1+1+1)=5,
$$

and both local components equal $\ln(32/27)$ nats. Their signed difference
is zero. At key `03+05+06`, the ideal contains only singleton patterns.
All four occupied patterns are covered, so $w=4$ and all three singleton
increments are zero; that local component is zero in both channels.

These are local values at the named anchor, not averages. At each other
anchor, key `07` has a missing maximal pair pattern and its component vanishes.
The averaged `07` components are therefore $\tfrac14\ln(32/27)$ each.
The constant target makes both channels identical at every anchor: all signed
cumulatives and atoms, and source-target MI, are zero. This hand example checks
component algebra, not predictive information or an executed implementation.

## Averaging all 108 coordinates and counting the work

Let $\rho_\alpha^+(\omega)=\exp(\pi_\alpha^+(\omega))$ and
$\rho_\alpha^-(\omega)=\exp(\pi_\alpha^-(\omega))$ denote the explicit rational
ratios above; no numerical exponential is needed. The averaged component atom is

$$
\overline\pi_\alpha^\pm=
\frac1N\ln\left[\prod_{\omega:c_\omega>0}
             (\rho_\alpha^\pm(\omega))^{c_\omega}\right].
$$

Weights are joint counts, not uniform occupied-cell weights. An averaged
component is zero iff every occupied local component is zero. Averaged net
sign and zero require comparing the two weighted rational products, not local
positive gaps.

Retain the 54 cumulative products: at each of 18 keys they are
$\prod_{\omega:c_\omega>0}(N/U_\alpha(\omega))^{c_\omega}$,
$\prod_{\omega:c_\omega>0}(N_t/V_\alpha(\omega))^{c_\omega}$, and their quotient.
Together with the 54 atom products, these are the original **108** coordinates,
not 18 signs or 108 independent atoms. Each reported average divides its
logarithm by $N$. The `07` cumulatives are joint source entropy, conditional
source entropy given $T$, and MI; each sums all 18 corresponding atoms.

One scan per anchor builds all eight agreement counts. With $m$ occupied joint
cells and $W$ bounding primitive state-comparison work per tuple, scanning costs
$O(m^2W)$ comparisons. Counting, integer bit costs, normalization, weighted
products, logarithms and output storage remain separate.

Let $b=\operatorname{bitlen}(N)$ be the binary digit count of $N$.
Raw weighted atom-component numerators and
denominators each have loose ceiling $4Nb$ bits; atom net has $8Nb$ bits.
Each ratio numerator or denominator has at most four event-count factors,
each at most $N$.
These are symbolic bounds, not runtime, heap or speedup measurements; $N$ can be
enormous relative to $b$. The unchanged
[revision 1 resource policy](../claims/SX-CERTIFIED-AVERAGED-PID3-001/bindings.md)
requires normalized per-coordinate and full 108 aggregate plans before powers;
these limits and costs are unchanged.
