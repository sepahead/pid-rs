# Constant-channel occupancy reduction: exact error and a negative result

12 October 2026. Sepehr Mahmoudian.

Replacing the two motion channels by constants does **not** preserve this recording's
categorical shared-exclusions PID within 0.0033 nats. The largest error among all
108 averaged expressions is greater than one-third nat. At least one signed-net
atom is more than 0.01 nat larger before collapse than after it. These conclusions hold for
every finite retained-source encoding consistent with the same motion/target table.

Status: native integer counts plus model-agent-reviewed hand derivations.
No formal proof, complete three-source PID execution, population confidence,
held-out prediction gain, Rust refinement or qualified PDF is claimed.
Original SxPID3 Programs A–E remain 0/5 fully closed.

## 1. Research question, data and glossary

Question: can we replace a low-activity channel pair by constants and avoid a
full three-source decomposition while guaranteeing small allocation error?
We test that reduction, not whether motion sensors should be removed from a deployed
system. A good predictor may tolerate a change that does not preserve PID allocations.

The real problem is estimating room occupancy from non-intrusive measurements.
[UCI 864](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/864/room%2Boccupancy%2Bestimation)
documents binary passive-infrared motion signals and a manually recorded occupant count.
Dataset credit: Singh and Chaudhari, *Room Occupancy Estimation* (2018),
[DOI 10.24432/C5P605](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5P605), CC BY 4.0.

This study uses the finite categorical shared-exclusions measure of
[Makkeh, Gutknecht and Wibral (MGW), source v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2002.03356v5),
especially its event definition, averaging and component reconstruction.
It does not use continuous Ehrlich PID or Williams–Beer I_min. The
[entropy-bound note](SXPID_CONSTANT_CHANNEL_ENTROPY_BOUND_2026_10_12.md)
gives the complete upper-bound proof and its published component-positivity dependency.
The exact-error identity below is a repository-derived corollary, not a priority claim.

Before formulas:

| Term or symbol | Meaning here |

| Categorical law | Probabilities on fixed finite labels, with no numerical distance between labels |

| \(P_6,P_7\) | Physical PIR columns S6_PIR and S7_PIR, each labeled 0 or 1 |
| \(C=(P_6,P_7)\) | Joint motion tuple, with labels 00, 01, 10, 11 |
| \(c_0=(0,0)\) | Fixed tuple used for collapse; 00 means no reported motion, not no people |
| \(X\) | Retained finite source; its encoder is not fixed by this count-only screen |
| \(T\) | Original Room_Occupancy_Count target, with labels 0, 1, 2, 3; no target recoding for PID |
| \(q\) | Collapsed law: replace \(C\) by \(c_0\) in every row, preserving the full joint law of \(X,T\) |
| \(H(C),H(C\mid T)\) | Joint motion entropy and its target-conditioned entropy |
| \(I(C;T)\) | Motion/target mutual information; \(H(C)-H(C\mid T)\) |
| \(F_\beta^+,F_\beta^-,F_\beta^{\rm net}\) | Averaged informative, misinformative and signed-net cumulative at node \(\beta\) |
| \(\Pi_\alpha^u\) | Averaged atom at node \(\alpha\), component \(u\in\{+,-,\mathrm{net}\}\) |
| Antichain/node \(\alpha,\beta\) | Nonempty family of nonempty source-index subsets (branches), none containing another |
| \(E_{\rm full}\) | Largest absolute \(p\)-versus-\(q\) difference among all 108 averaged expressions |
| \(M_{\rm net}\) | Largest absolute difference among the 18 signed-net atoms only |
| Nats, \(\ln\) | Information unit using the natural logarithm |

An encoder applied to the recording's rows gives one empirical extension \(p\).
The proof also allows finite extensions not represented by those \(N\) rows;
only their \(C,T\) marginal is fixed. No \(X\) encoder was selected or executed.
Entropy uses \(H(C)=-\sum_c p_C(c)\ln p_C(c)\), with \(0\ln0=0\).

The three mathematical sources are source 1=\(X\), source 2=\(P_6\), source 3=\(P_7\).
Physical column numbers 6 and 7 are not lattice indices.
There is no Gaussian assumption or independence assumption between channels.
Row independence is unnecessary for a deterministic comparison of finite laws.
Population or held-out conclusions require additional sampling assumptions.

Collapse retains **all** rows. Filtering to 00 rows would instead change the
\(X,T\) marginal and answer a different question.

## 3. Why the full error is exactly joint nuisance entropy

A shared-exclusions event requires agreement on every source within at least one
branch of its node: AND inside a branch, OR across branches.

Take the node \(\beta_C=\{\{2,3\}\}\): **one branch containing both motion sources**.
Its anchor agreement event is exactly \(C=C(z)\), where \(z\) is the supported
source/target realization being evaluated. It is not an OR of the two separate
motion equalities.

Let \(p_C(c)\) and \(p_T(t)\) be marginal probabilities of tuple \(c\) and target
\(t\); \(p(c,t)\) is their joint probability. At a supported anchor, the local
plus value is \(-\ln p_C(c)\) and the local minus value is
\(-\ln[p(c,t)/p_T(t)]\). Averaging with the actual joint-law weights gives

\[
F_{\beta_C}^+(p)=H(C),\qquad
F_{\beta_C}^-(p)=H(C\mid T),\qquad
F_{\beta_C}^{\rm net}(p)=I(C;T).
\]

Under \(q\), the complete tuple is constant. Its agreement event has probability
one, also conditional on every supported target. Thus all three cumulatives at
this node are zero. The plus coordinate alone gives \(E_{\rm full}\ge H(C)\).

The reviewed entropy theorem bounds every averaged component/net cumulative
and atom difference by \(H(C)\). Combining the two inequalities yields

\[
\boxed{E_{\rm full}=H(C).}
\]

This is true for every finite compatible law of \(X,C,T\), not just a special
sharpness example. There are 18 nodes, two roles (cumulative and atom), and
three components: \(18\cdot2\cdot3=108\) expressions.
The full maximum adds **no new scalar beyond Shannon entropy**; its contribution
is a guarantee about the complete MGW allocation vector.

This identity does not apply to \(M_{\rm net}\) alone. For example, let the two
motion sources copy a fair bit, \(X\) be constant and \(T\) be constant. Four
equally weighted rows, two at motion 00 and two at 11, give \(H(C)=\ln2\).
All net cumulatives and atoms are zero before and after collapse, but
\(E_{\rm full}=\ln2\). Plus and minus are not target information by themselves.

## 4. An exact entropy enclosure without numerical logs

Let \(\varepsilon=1198/10129\) be the empirical departure probability.
The notation \(1[\text{condition}]\) is one when the condition is true, zero otherwise.
Let \(D=1[C\ne00]\) be the departure indicator.
For a binary probability \(0<v<1\), define
\(h_2(v)=-v\ln v-(1-v)\ln(1-v)\), with zero at either endpoint.
Because \(D\) is determined by \(C\), the entropy chain rule gives
\(H(C)=h_2(\varepsilon)+\varepsilon H(C\mid D=1)\).
Here \(H(C\mid D=0)=0\); the other conditional law has at most three labels,
so its entropy is at most \(\ln3\). Therefore

\[
h_2(\varepsilon)\le H(C)\le h_2(\varepsilon)+\varepsilon\ln3.
\]

Here is a short rational enclosure, with every inequality direction explicit.

1. \(8\cdot1198<10129<16\cdot1198\), so
   \(1/16<\varepsilon<1/8\) and \(\ln(1/\varepsilon)>3\ln2\).
2. The positive log series gives \(\ln2\ge2/3\).
   Also \(-\ln(1-\varepsilon)\ge\varepsilon\).
   Therefore \(H(C)\ge h_2(\varepsilon)>2\varepsilon+
   \varepsilon(1-\varepsilon)=3\varepsilon-\varepsilon^2\).
3. Exact substitution gives
   \[
   3\varepsilon-\varepsilon^2
   =34968422/102596641>1/3,
   \]
   since \(3\cdot34968422=104905266>102596641\).
4. For the upper side, \(-(1-\varepsilon)\ln(1-\varepsilon)\le\varepsilon\).
   This follows from \(\ln x\le x-1\) at \(x=1/(1-\varepsilon)\).
   Thus \(H(C)\le\varepsilon[1+\ln(3/\varepsilon)]\).
5. \(3/\varepsilon=30387/1198<32\), so
   \(\ln(3/\varepsilon)<5\ln2\).
   With \(w=1/3\), the classical positive series is
   \[
   \ln2=2\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{w^{2k+1}}{2k+1}.
   \]
   Here \(k\) is the nonnegative series index. The terms after \(k=0\)
   sum to at most \(2w^3/[3(1-w^2)]=1/36\). Hence \(\ln2\le25/36\).
   See [DLMF 4.6.E4](https://dlmf.nist.gov/4.6.E4) for the series.
6. Consequently \(H(C)<96439/182322<53/100\), with
   \(100\cdot96439=9643900<53\cdot182322=9663066\).

Therefore

\[
\boxed{1/3<E_{\rm full}=H(C)<53/100\ \text{nats}.}
\]

This is not merely a failed small-error certificate: the actual full-vector
error exceeds the illustrative tolerance \(\tau=33/10000=0.0033\) nats.
No numerical entropy value or outward-log implementation was executed.

An easier independent check uses \(-\ln x\ge1-x\):
\(H(C)\ge1-\sum_c p_C(c)^2=22327550/102596641>1/5\).
The sum of squared counts is 80269091; \(N^2=102596641\).
Both derivations reject the small-error proposal.

## 5. A signed-net atom must also change: every step

The full norm might seem irrelevant when only signed target-information atoms
are wanted. This recording supplies a separate net-atom obstruction.

Define two **proof-only** binary variables:
\(A=1[C\ne00]\) indicates motion, \(B=1[T\ne0]\) indicates recorded presence.
They do not replace the PID target \(T\).
Their table is \((A=0,B=0):8204\), \((0,1):727\),
\((1,0):24\), \((1,1):1174\).

Let \(a=p(A=1)=1198/N\), \(b=p(B=1)=1901/N\), and
\(d=p(A=1,B=1)-ab\), the binary covariance. Then

\[
d=\frac{1174N-1198\cdot1901}{N^2}
 =\frac{9614048}{102596641}>\frac9{100},
\]
because \(961404800>923369769\). Also \(0<a<1/8\).

### Grouping cannot increase relative entropy

For positive finite laws \(L,K\), define relative entropy
\(D_{\rm KL}(L\Vert K)=\sum_i L_i\ln(L_i/K_i)\), where \(i\) labels cells.
For a nonempty cell group \(G\), write \(L_G=\sum_{i\in G}L_i\),
\(K_G=\sum_{i\in G}K_i\), and normalized weights \(w_i=K_i/K_G\).
Convexity of \(y\ln y\) gives

\[
\sum_{i\in G}L_i\ln(L_i/K_i)
=K_G\sum_{i\in G}w_i (L_i/K_i)\ln(L_i/K_i)
\ge L_G\ln(L_G/K_G).
\]

The variable \(y>0\) is the argument of the convex function.
Its second derivative is \(1/y>0\), establishing convexity.
Sum over groups. Apply this with \(L=p_{CT}\), \(K=p_Cp_T\), grouped by
\(A,B\). All 16 cells here are positive. The product law groups to \(p_Ap_B\),
so \(I(C;T)\ge I(A;B)\). These are classical log-sum/data-processing facts;
the argument above states the needed positive-law case.

### Binary curvature yields a rational lower bound

For fixed \(0<b<1\), define the binary relative entropy

\[
f_b(x)=x\ln(x/b)+(1-x)\ln[(1-x)/(1-b)],\quad 0<x<1.
\]

Here \(x\) is a binary probability, not the retained source \(X\).
Differentiate: \(f_b(b)=f_b'(b)=0\) and
\(f_b''(x)=1/[x(1-x)]\ge4\), because \(x(1-x)\le1/4\).
The function \(f_b(x)-2(x-b)^2\) is convex and has value and derivative zero
at \(b\); hence \(f_b(x)\ge2(x-b)^2\) on **both** sides of \(b\).

Let \(r_1=p(B=1\mid A=1)=1174/1198=b+d/a\) and
\(r_0=p(B=1\mid A=0)=727/8931=b-d/(1-a)\).
Both are strictly between zero and one. Expanding the binary joint relative
entropy by its two \(A\) rows gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
I(C;T)&\ge I(A;B)=a f_b(r_1)+(1-a)f_b(r_0)\\
&\ge2d^2[1/a+1/(1-a)]
 =\frac{2d^2}{a(1-a)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Since \(0<a<1/8\),
\(7/64-a(1-a)=(1/8-a)(7/8-a)>0\).
Therefore

\[
\boxed{I(C;T)>\frac{128}{7}(9/100)^2=\frac{648}{4375}\ \text{nats}.}
\]

This is a lower bound, not the exact MI or an optimal bound.
A different diagonal-group argument gives the weaker \(I(C;T)>81/1250\);
a normalized rational-tilt argument gives \(I(C;T)>1/40\).
They share the same table, not independent datasets.
No log was numerically evaluated and no statistical independence test was run.

### Thirteen atoms reconstruct the nuisance cumulative

The MGW order is \(\alpha\preceq\beta\) if every branch of \(\beta\)
contains some branch of \(\alpha\). Reconstruction is
\(F_\beta^{\rm net}=\sum_{\alpha\preceq\beta}\Pi_\alpha^{\rm net}\).

For \(\beta_C=\{\{2,3\}\}\), failure of this condition means every branch
of \(\alpha\) contains source 1. There are precisely five such nodes:
\(\{\{1\}\}\), \(\{\{1,2\}\}\), \(\{\{1,3\}\}\),
\(\{\{1,2,3\}\}\), and \(\{\{1,2\},\{1,3\}\}\).
Thus 13 of the 18 nodes are below or equal to \(\beta_C\).
The [complete keyed carrier](../claims/SX-CERTIFIED-AVERAGED-PID3-001/conventions.md#the-complete-18-node-carrier)
also permits a direct check; no implementation array order is assumed.

Under \(q\), a branch omitting source 1 is certain; if all branches contain
source 1, their OR event is just \(X=x\). Thus the cumulative vector is zero
unless \(\{\{1\}\}\preceq\beta\), when it is \(I(X;T)\).
This is exactly the reconstruction column for the retained-source atom
\(\{\{1\}\}\); unique inversion leaves every other atom zero.
The retained node is outside these 13. Subtracting the two reconstruction identities,

\[
\sum_{\alpha\preceq\beta_C}
 [\Pi_\alpha^{\rm net}(p)-\Pi_\alpha^{\rm net}(q)]=I(C;T).
\]

At least one signed summand is at least the sum divided by 13.
Each \(q\) summand is zero. It follows that, for some node in this set,

\[
\boxed{\Pi_\alpha^{\rm net}(p)-\Pi_\alpha^{\rm net}(q)
 >\frac{648}{56875}>\frac1{100}\ \text{nat}.}
\]

The last comparison is exact: \(64800>56875\).
Negative net atoms are allowed; none was clamped. Positivity of every signed atom
is not a premise. This existence proof holds for **any** finite \(X\) compatible
with the same empirical \(C,T\) table. It does not identify the failing node or
calculate the complete three-source PID.

## 6. Prediction loss is a different question

The nuisance-node discrepancy is \(I(C;T)\).
The loss of total source MI is instead
\(I(X,C;T)-I(X;T)=I(C;T\mid X)\).
These need not agree. A mathematical extension \(X=T\) makes the latter zero,
while the nuisance-node and atom obstructions above remain.
This is a counterexample to a prediction-loss inference, **not** a suggestion to
leak the target into a deployable feature.

Two simple in-record comparators show why direct task evaluation matters:

- Predict presence iff any motion is reported: 9378/10129 binary labels match,
  versus 8228/10129 for always-unoccupied. The motion rule misses 727/1901
  occupied rows and gives 24 false positives among 8228 unoccupied rows.
- For the original four-class target, table-fitted modal predictions
  \(00\mapsto0,01\mapsto3,10\mapsto2,11\mapsto3\) match 8817/10129 labels.
  Always predicting zero matches 8228/10129.

These are descriptive same-recording comparisons, not held-out gains, calibration
or state-of-the-art comparisons. The modal rule uses evaluation labels.
Hard zero/one forecasts have infinite log loss on their errors, so their
accuracy must not be described as finite proper-log-loss performance.

The recording has seven calendar-date groups, spanning 2017/12/22 to 2018/01/11.
Observed intervals range from 30 to 1318547 seconds; timezone is unknown.
The provider's nominal cadence/four-day description does not establish IID rows
or explain these date groups. Causes remain unadjudicated; no rows were trimmed.
The fixed-IID zero-departure procedure in the entropy note must abstain here:
1198 departures occur, and IID is not established.

[UCI 357](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/357/occupancy%2Bdetection)
has no matching native PIR pair. It is an applicability negative, not a second
execution of this experiment. A separately fitted categorical encoding would
define a different study. Neural/imaging, embodied and world-model applications
still need justified inputs, multiple datasets, simple comparators and task evidence.

## 7. Rust implementation and computational cost

No Rust API, kernel, feature or tolerance changed. The default
stable categorical discrete_sxpid2/3/n APIs in
[the Rust source](../crates/pid-core/src/sxpid.rs) implement empirical MGW PID
for two through four sources. No public collapse-norm helper, certified entropy
engine or learner is asserted here. See the
[engineering baseline](../RUST_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_COST.md).

The reference counter scans \(N\) rows of 19 fields, counting a fixed 4-by-4 table.
Parsing timestamps, hashing/capturing input twice and serializing output add work.
Its 4 MiB raw-input and 16 KiB output caps are source admission checks, not hard
OS memory/deadline guarantees. Raw, decoded and serialized buffers coexist;
memory scales with input bytes plus fixed count/date records.
Exact rational comparisons require integer bit costs growing with \(\log N\).

For general nuisance tuple width \(d_C\) and \(k_C\) occupied labels, an ordered
count map costs \(O(Nd_C\log(k_C+1))\) comparisons and \(O(k_Cd_C)\) label
storage plus count storage. This assumes bounded-size label comparisons;
arbitrary labels add their encoding/bit cost. These are source-level costs, not timings.
The full-vector maximum needs only nuisance counts, not the complete PID lattice.
The net obstruction needs the 16 \(C,T\) counts; it still does not recover all atoms.

Full PID, preprocessing, held-out model inference, training and resampling have
additional costs and their own resource/cancellation contracts. No latency,
RSS benchmark, real-time deployment or formal executable refinement is reported.
Python is used for reference counting, not a production runtime replacement.

## 8. Review, corrections and remaining evidence

Three model-agent lenses separately assessed vector scope, exact entropy and
the MI lower bound. Initial artifacts were frozen before their stated exposure;
later root hints and post-freeze critique were recorded. The root checked the
actual tables, source, carrier, proof steps and integer arithmetic.
The root derivation/data analysis is exploratory, not a repaired preregistration
or candidate-inaccessible confirmation. Common paper/event/count dependencies
remain; model agreement is neither a kernel proof nor independent human review.

Earlier private drafts described only certificate failure and suggested actual
PID error could be zero. That is false for the full 108-expression norm and false
for preserving all 18 net atoms on this table. The preimages are retained with
a rejection disposition. Constant-target zero-net examples remain valid;
they cannot be transferred to the full-component norm.

The original tolerances, flags, kernels and control rosters are unchanged.
Stock formal qualification, exact theorem encoding, complete executable/Rust
correspondence, population coverage, matched multi-dataset task tests,
qualified PDF and accepted-mainline publication remain open.
