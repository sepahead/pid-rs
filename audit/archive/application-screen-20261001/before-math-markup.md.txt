# Application screening for shared exclusions and bounds

Author: Sepehr Mahmoudian

This first screen covers selected existing and recent mathematics, bounds and ecosystem interfaces. It identifies useful safeguards and two possible study directions, neither run. No new task improvement, state-of-the-art result or scientific priority is established.

The PID lane is finite categorical Makkeh–Gutknecht–Wibral (MGW) shared exclusions. Its defining source is [Makkeh, Gutknecht and Wibral (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.103.032149). All values here use natural logarithms and nats. Continuous Ehrlich shared exclusions, KSG mutual-information estimation and other PID definitions remain separate. Quantizing an input changes the estimand. A contextual law requires positive context probability. Dependence between sources is allowed; independence of complete rows is a separate sampling premise.

## What established and recent mathematics can support

Each use below is conditional, not a validated application. Full statements and derivations remain in the linked repository reports.

| Result | Plausible use | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed-alphabet averaged-MGW continuity | Bound how an averaged atom can change when the encoded law changes, including rare cells entering support. | Keep the complete alphabet, event map and full Möbius inverse fixed. Supply a justified total-variation radius. Use monotone upper envelopes, not unchecked substitution into nonmonotone exact formulas. Exact-real sensitivity is not a confidence interval or binary64 certificate. |
| Finite-prefix mean-bias bounds | Plan the horizon of the specific joined-mismatch and target-retained-rank experiment when an MGW atom is already the chosen target. | Complete rows are iid from one finite law. Use a fresh anchor, fixed raw horizon and fixed atom. Population support or probability floors need justification. This is not the ordinary row plug-in API, sampling variance or stopping coverage. |
| Dependency-color concentration | Account for overlapping rows when a known construction gives mutually independent complete rows within each declared class. | Declare the coloring before outcomes. Mere spacing, distinct seeds or color labels do not prove independence. Any transfer to atoms needs its stated continuity/support premises. No automatic valid-color inference is implemented. |
| Recent finite-event decoder result | Check whether inserting a decoded full-source match changes an MGW source event or its two cumulative components. | Finite normalized nonnegative law, positive anchor, nonempty families and a supported decoder for every residual source coordinate. Twenty-six stock-verified theorem statements do not establish full PID3 inversion, zero atoms, calibration or population determinism from a fitted decoder. |
| Older KSG harmonic range | Provide an arithmetic regression oracle for eligible positive-integer digamma terms. | Require n≥2, 1≤k<n, k≤x,y≤n and the stated digamma premise. The core is scoped; integration remains NO-GO. No MGW-atom bound, neighbor-geometry guarantee or estimator calibration follows. |

For the prefix experiment, write $B_h$ for its block statistic and $\theta$ for the fixed population atom. For any member source collection $C$ of the chosen lattice node, let $K_{CT}$ count positive population cells of its source-target projection. The established mean-error bound is

$$
\left|\theta-\mathbb E[B_h]\right|\le\frac{K_{CT}-1}{h+1}.
$$

Here $h+1$ counts the anchor and further complete iid rows. An observed minimum frequency cannot supply a population floor. Simulated ticks and restored siblings cannot be substituted for independent rows without a sampling argument.

Read the [continuity derivation](../../main-milestones/shared-exclusions-audit-20260930/SUPPORT_CHANGE_TOLERANT_AVERAGED_SXPID_CONTINUITY.md), [prefix statistic and bias derivation](../../main-milestones/shared-exclusions-audit-20260930/audit/formal/lean-prefix-mgw-bias/EXPOSITION.md), [dependency assumptions](../../main-milestones/shared-exclusions-audit-20260930/DEPENDENCY_COLORED_SXPID_CONCENTRATION.md) and [KSG disposition](../../main-milestones/shared-exclusions-audit-20260930/claims/KSG-INTEGER-HARMONIC-001/integration-disposition-v4.md). Their formal, handwritten, executable and open obligations remain distinct.

## Existing office occupancy evidence remains negative

The repository already evaluates recorded light and CO₂ against binary office occupancy with four training-fitted bins per source. Each recording defines its own empirical law on at most 32 cells. Time-series rows are not declared iid. The later recording has positive MGW synergy largely cancelled by negative unique CO₂ contribution. The tested source-matching-union predictor had worse probability scores than direct baselines. This is a useful warning, not a general rejection of PID or CO₂ sensing. No building-level generalization or PID-guided improvement is established. See the [recorded study and comparisons](../../main-milestones/shared-exclusions-audit-20260930/audit/evidence/real-occupancy-sensors-example-2026-09-08.md) and [UCI problem source](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/357/occupancy+detection).

## Crebain sensor retention is an unrun candidate

NCP means Neuro-Cybernetic Protocol. It owns transport and buffer contracts, not neural computation or scientific validity. Crebain's optional [sensor contract](https://github.com/sepahead/crebain/tree/main/integrations/ncp-force-ground-sensors) exposes timed RGB and pressure observations. Its [family contract](https://github.com/sepahead/crebain/blob/main/integrations/ncp-force-ground-sensors/FAMILY.md) defines a future three-tick pressure-RMS target. The reported E1 forecast remains null or inconclusive, not PID benefit.

Proposed context $c$ fixes the simulator profile, scene, cameras, microphone, continuation action, landmark and horizon. Let $S_R$ be a training-fitted binary bin of pre-landmark RGB change in one fixed region. Let $S_A$ bin prior pressure RMS. Let $T$ bin the later pressure RMS in pascals. Freeze feature arithmetic, thresholds, edge/tie rules, missingness and clipping policy before evaluation. The object is $P(S_R,S_A,T\mid C=c)$, with binary alphabets and at most eight cells. It describes encoded simulator features, not calibrated analog sensors or a physical drone.

Start with one row per prospectively independent episode. Keep restored siblings, repeated continuations and overlapping windows together in every split. Inputs must end before target availability. Check source and availability ticks and retain absent cells explicitly. Reject collapsed bins, broken synchronization, insufficient support or leakage.

The decision is whether RGB adds enough forecasting value to justify its acquisition cost. Compare constant, RGB-only, acoustic-only and joint forecasts using held-out log loss or Brier score. Compare MI, conditional MI and modality ablations. A training-only, predeclared PID-assisted selector must improve that decision against a matched selector without PID. Otherwise retain the null result. No current selector, prospective evidence or real-flight benefit is claimed.

## Brain imaging is an unrun candidate with additional gates

[ZAPBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.02618v1) defines roughly thirty-second forecasting from calcium imaging. It provides a real context-length and representation-budget problem beyond spike counting. A proposed study would bin fixed recent and distant activity summaries as $S_1,S_2$ and a future regional summary as $T$. The conditional law must fix its stimulus/session context. If binary PID selects context for the original forecasting task, held-out trace MAE and measured cost remain the task endpoints. Binary event prediction would instead be a different task.

Do not use a pointwise PID value requiring the unknown target as an online selector. Group stimulus repeats and purge overlapping raw and preprocessing windows. The published normalization uses a 400-step temporal baseline window, so causal availability needs separate inspection. Remaining temporal dependence still needs an uncertainty model. One fish gives no cross-animal inference. Taxis is withheld from benchmark training; a taxis-specific training law cannot be manufactured from test labels.

Public [ZAPBench datasets](https://zapbench-release.storage.googleapis.com/volumes/README.html) state CC-BY 4.0. [Fish Fire&Wire](https://www.janelia.org/fish-firewire) is different: approved group access, no redistribution, versioned incomplete snapshots and no submission using its data before December 1, 2027. No access request or data use occurred here. A static connectome alone does not supply a functional source-target law. The user-linked [MindStudio article](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/fruit-fly-brain-connectome-ai-training) does not establish a PID benefit; its email-classifier claim remains unverified in this screen.

## A rejected selector is only conditional mutual information

Fix one finite law, context, encoded sources and target. Define $I_1=I(S_1;T)$, $I_2=I(S_2;T)$ and $I_{12}=I(S_1,S_2;T)$ under that same contextual law. Let $R$ be MGW redundancy. The two-source definitions give

$$
U_2=I_2-R,\qquad
\mathrm{Syn}=I_{12}-I_1-I_2+R.
$$

Add the expressions without clamping any signed term:

$$
U_2+\mathrm{Syn}
=(I_2-R)+(I_{12}-I_1-I_2+R)
=I_{12}-I_1
=I(S_2;T\mid S_1).
$$

The first cancellation is exact scalar algebra; the last equality is the finite-law Shannon chain rule. Thus a selector using this sum is a CMI selector, not evidence of extra MGW value. This identity is already used in the recorded office study. Shipped Z3 4.16.0 returned unsat for the scalar counterexample obligation and sat for its deliberately false control. Both commands returned zero with empty stderr. The sources and captures remain beside this report. This check does not prove the probability interpretation, estimator accuracy or application benefit. A genuine MGW-dependent rule still needs a predeclared scientific rationale and matched held-out test.

No inspected local matching consumer is adopted. Absence in those checkouts is not external nonexistence. Do not equate joint video/action prediction with an interventional planner.

The inspected Prisoma revision retains an older offline action-target screen, same-row fitting warnings and a missing typed source-target ancestry route. Modern integrations linked by NCP are absent from that local checkout. These are revision-local blockers, not global claims about Prisoma. A future physical-outcome study needs a target-specific prediction landmark, tensor ancestry, explicit units, episode-level splits and matched input access. A representation already conditioned on the proposal cannot be used as a source for predicting that same proposal. No weights were downloaded, consumer pin changed or physical actuator controlled.

Formally checked arithmetic and finite-law identities remain valuable even when an application fails. They do not establish novelty or deployment value.
