# Open failure: no compiled exact-versus-Neumaier guard discriminator

## What the retained witness proves

For

```text
I1 = 0x4072352e28889826
I2 = 0x404488b4ebc88f45
J  = 0x3ff843725bb2f39b
R  = 0x404281666d288d41
Syn = 0xc0725dd48600e573
```

the exact represented-atom reconstruction is five ordered binary64 positions from `J`, whereas
ordinary left-to-right addition is 155 positions away. The constructor accepts under the exact
guard, so a compiled regression can kill a sequential-reconstruction mutation.

## What it does not prove

Neumaier compensated reconstruction also lands five ordered positions from `J`. The witness does
not distinguish the production exact accumulator from `compensated_sum`. Targeted and random
searches have not found a quadruple whose exact and Neumaier reconstructions straddle the fixed
threshold 32.

Failure to find such a witness is not an equivalence theorem. Revision 3 therefore keeps the
compiled Neumaier-guard mutation obligation open. A textual checker can require the production
helper name and reject a source mutation, but that is source correspondence evidence, not a
compiled behavioral kill.

## Reopen and closure condition

Close this gap only with either:

1. a retained finite input whose public constructor outcome differs under exact and Neumaier guard
   accumulation; or
2. a proof, with checked binary64 boundary cases, that Neumaier and exact reconstruction induce the
   same threshold predicate on the complete constructor domain.

The second route must include non-finite intermediates, overflow, subnormals, signed zero, sign
crossings, and every ordered-distance boundary; a search result cannot substitute for it.
