# Retained dependency failure: `exp0` repeated the historical direct formula

## Finding

The pre-correction `exp0` diagnostic computed optional synergy independently as

```text
mi_s1s2_t - mi_s1_t - mi_s2_t + redundancy
```

instead of calling `Pid2Result::from_estimate`. Consequently, changing the checked constructor
alone would leave the diagnostic on a different represented-input arithmetic and rejection
contract.

## Dependency reach and correction

The bypass reaches the catalog method `validation.exp0` and any summary/run-log field populated by
that optional outcome. It does not change KSG neighbor counts or the redundancy estimate.

The correction must construct `Pid2Estimate`, call `Pid2Result::from_estimate`, return the checked
`synergy` on success, and map `NumericalInstability` to the diagnostic's existing explicit
abstention state. Other typed errors are impossible for finite four-scalar construction and must
not be silently converted into a value.

## Regression and residual boundary

A source mutation must reject restoration of the direct formula, while existing `exp0` smoke,
strict-band, summary, and run-log tests must pass. Routing through the constructor supplies
arithmetic and typed-error consistency; it does not turn `exp0` into estimator validation or a
generic pass/fail scientific gate.
