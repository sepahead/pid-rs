## Historical behavior

The historical expression

```text
((J - I1) - I2) + R
```

returns bits `0x3c70000000000000`, or `+2^-56`. Exchanging only `I1,I2` makes the same expression
return positive-zero bits `0x0000000000000000`. Both atom tuples satisfy the constructor's
existing 32-ordered-bit identity guard, so its exact-sum fallback is not selected.

## Correction and residual boundary

The proposed correction computes `exact_binary64_sum([J,-I1,-I2,R])` unconditionally after the
finite-input check, while preserving unique-atom arithmetic and the final identity guard. The
fixed bits make the arithmetic counterexample platform independent. They do not prove that the
quadruple is attainable from a valid continuous population, correctly estimated, or statistically
meaningful.
