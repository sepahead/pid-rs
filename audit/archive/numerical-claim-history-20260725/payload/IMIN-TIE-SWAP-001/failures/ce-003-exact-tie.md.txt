# CE-003 — exact empirical tie with unequal binary64 branch values

## Table and exact derivation

The count vector is `[0,0,0,1,1,2,3,0]` in lexicographic `(S1,S2,T)` order, with `N=7` and target
value `t=1`. Its positive rows are

```text
(0,1,1) x 1
(1,0,0) x 1
(1,0,1) x 2
(1,1,0) x 3.
```

For `S1`, the `(source marginal, source-target)` counts at `t=1` are `(1,1)` and `(6,2)`.
For `S2`, they are `(3,2)` and `(4,1)`. Since `c_t=3`,

```text
i(S1;1) = (1/3)log(7/3)  + (2/3)log(7/9),
i(S2;1) = (2/3)log(14/9) + (1/3)log(7/12).
```

Multiplying by three and combining exact logarithms gives the same rational product `343/243`.
The cross multiplication is `343*972 = 1372*243 = 333396`.
