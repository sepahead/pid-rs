## Mathematical scope

Two targets concern a real function's restricted Bochner integral divided by
set volume. Assumptions: a measurable set; finite restricted measure; positive
real set volume; integrability on that set; positive anchor; nonnegative envelope
width; and an almost-everywhere two-sided exponential bound under the restricted
measure. The ambient measure need not be finite. Four true controls distinguish
empty volume, constant density and missing upper or lower bounds.

The integral has function-times-measure units. Its normalized mean and anchor
have function units; their log ratio is dimensionless and uses the natural log.
The raw mean is not an information quantity. No probability normalization,
density representation, independent sampling or estimator calibration follows.
