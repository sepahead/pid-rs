## Disposition

recorded defining-paper and executable boundaries. The source establishes
for every real a,b with 0<r=|a|+|b|<=1/2 and M=8:

A=J-Q-D+2P >= a^2/8+b^2/4 >= r^2/12 > 0,

2. **Own-law mean.** The uniform torus reference is a device for integration;
   it is not substituted into the defining PID. The actual v law has

3. **Top lower cut.** Each proper antichain contains a branch extendable to
   a pair, so the union of the three pair lower sets covers exactly the
   seventeen proper positions. Their intersections are the corresponding
   multi-pair lower sets. Signed inclusion-exclusion therefore gives the
   seven-cut formula and Q+D-2P. No atom positivity was assumed.
4. **Joint integral and series.** The explicit integer inverse verifies the
   torus change of variables; v,u are independent under the uniform
   reference. |Y|<=r<1 gives uniform absolute convergence. The simultaneous
   half-period shift cancels all odd moments. Independent cosine constant
   coefficients give the factorial formula and nonnegative even terms.
5. **Extra harmonic lower bound.** Subtracting J(a,0) cancels j=m at each
   order; all remaining terms are nonnegative, beginning with b^2/4.
   The author's curvature cross-check also works: sign-pairing converts
   E[E^2/(1+aC+tE)] to E[E^2/(1-(aC+tE)^2)]>=1/2; independence yields
   J_b(a,0)=0. Taylor's integral remainder handles both signs of b.
6. **Proper-term lower bound.** The direct derivative identity follows by
   algebra and E[C]=0; positive density gives R''<=0. Concavity at

   of each inequality is correct. No division by a or P is needed.
7. **Radius envelope.** The weighted-square identity is exact, with
   (|a|-2|b|)^2/24 on the right. The bound is uniform for real signs.
   Equality in this relaxation is not sharpness of A or of its error.
8. **Tail and relative ceiling.** The first omitted term has degree 2M+2

   exponent. Division uses the positive true A, not J or A_M. Monotonicity

9. **Examples and negatives.** The four tabulated lower bounds and rational

   pointwise-negative, zero-CMI-positive-atom and negative-a^6-coefficient

10. **Cost and transfer.** The source correctly separates forty-four moment
