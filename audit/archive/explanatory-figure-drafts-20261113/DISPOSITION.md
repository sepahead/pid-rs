## Reading boundary

Read this disposition before a payload. The original titles, descriptions, instructions,
formulas and success statements remain historical text. No current Markdown or publication
builder consumes these drafts. The bundle is recovery data, not an instruction to merge old
assets. Importing its objects requires the declared prerequisite and can use an isolated bare
repository. Any later checkout must be separate; it must not overwrite a dirty tree. This
archive does not supply the absent figure generators,
complete plot inputs or original execution records.

The mathematical object reviewed here is the finite categorical shared-exclusions functional
of Makkeh, Gutknecht and Wibral, on a fixed labelled alphabet and source grouping, at supported
anchors, with natural logarithms and information in nats. See the defining paper and the
[foundational audit](../../../FOUNDATIONAL_SHARED_EXCLUSIONS_PID_AUDIT.md). A *cumulative*
is a value indexed by a source antichain; a *net atom* is its signed Möbius inverse. An
*empirical law* is the PMF assigned by observed counts; treating it as a population for a
simulation does not identify the unknown recording population. The continuous KSG pipeline
has its own estimand and assumptions; categorical results do not qualify it.

## Corrections that govern any later use

| Draft | Finding and condition for reconsideration |
|---|---|
| `what-shared-exclusions-is` | The universal claim that no fixed auxiliary variable can carry the average is false. If the two sources and target are the same fair bit, redundancy is the mutual information of that bit with itself, `ln(2)`. The supported statement is **not generally** representable as MI with one fixed auxiliary variable. Category relabeling must be bijective; source exchange maps the source and atom keys and can exchange the two distinct unique values. |
| `order-reverses-implication` | Reflection from event inclusion to syntactic antichain order holds on all Boolean equality patterns. On one realized support it needs a full-pattern realization premise. With two constant sources, the singleton-source events coincide even though the singleton antichains are incomparable. Keep the Boolean domain or state the extra premise. |
| `event-to-atom-bounds` | `g_J(eta)` bounds the **common-mass logarithmic load**, with residual entropy handled separately; it does not bound an arbitrary event-probability change. Its endpoint value at total variation one is zero, while an event probability can change by one. The function is not monotone on the whole unit interval. Use the monotone envelope in the [support-change report](../../../SUPPORT_CHANGE_TOLERANT_AVERAGED_SXPID_CONTINUITY.md) when only a radius upper bound is known. |
| `plug-in-convergence` | Having observed every supported cell is not a finite-sample closeness certificate or a replacement for empirical-law convergence. State the sampling assumptions, eventual support stabilization and asymptotic convergence separately. An envelope above twenty displayed paths concerns those paths; the theorem's simultaneous probability statement has its own premises. See the [convergence report](../../../FINITE_ALPHABET_PLUGIN_CONVERGENCE.md). |
| `same-synergy-different-added-information` | The displayed constants require the primitive bits to be independent and fair and the functional to be the categorical MGW construction. Equal signed synergy does not imply equal additional Shannon information. |
| `third-source-budget` | Define the five-position absolute net-atom mass, conditional source entropy and added conditional MI before using their symbols. The displayed `2 H_bin(error)` Fano specialization requires a binary added source. A general alphabet of size `m` has the additional `2 error ln(m-1)` term under the corresponding decoder/error assumptions. The [current notes](../../../output/RESEARCH_NOTES_2026_10_10.md) retain the hand-derivation and component-positivity boundary. |
| `dependency-coloring-concentration` | The dependence premises and design factor must be explicit. Thirty exceedances in twenty thousand simulations, compared with a nominal seven, are a finite observation; they do not alone exactly refute a probabilistic inequality. The [dependency-color report](../../../DEPENDENCY_COLORED_SXPID_CONCENTRATION.md) governs the theorem. A rendered legend also clips at the right edge. |
| `theorems-on-a-real-population`, `continuity-routes`, `independent-label` and other simulation panels | Where a panel resamples binned recording counts, its assigned population is that finite empirical PMF, not the unknown recording population. Other panels use exact synthetic, specified drone/full-support or simulated spiking laws; each needs its own complete law and sampling specification. Recording dependence, estimator calibration and application benefit remain separate. New curves, seeds, counts, extrema and solver outputs need exact generators, input tables and execution records before adoption. |
| `exact-accumulation-order-invariance` | The witness concerns one fixed multiset of represented terms and one final rounding. Three binary64 increments separate the returned payload and the rounded reference; that is not exactly three ULPs from the real functional. Its exact reference is `ln(76441190400/29774625727)/12`. No general upstream rounding, source-exchange or real-functional exactness theorem follows. See [numerical assurance](../../../NUMERICAL_ASSURANCE.md). |
| `exact-atom-sign`, `ksg-term-exact-rational`, `ksg-neighbor-geometry` | Selected rational products and harmonic identities were independently recomputed. Reported MPFR intervals and binary64 bits still need their exact original evidence. Strict represented shell counts need positive finite radius, self exclusion and the stated shell assumptions. Local arithmetic does not close the current KSG claim or establish population support. |
| `same-total-different-structure`, `two-source-lattice-events`, `worked-and-gate` | The selected finite MGW arithmetic and event constructions are useful. Keep every law and pointwise versus averaged value explicit. Distinguish the OR-event posterior from a singleton-source posterior in the AND explanation. Comparisons with `I_min` and BROJA name different functionals and do not imply common atoms or a locally implemented BROJA solver. |
| `pipeline-and-refusals` | Its explicitly old code revision needs a current catalog/API comparison, including feature gates, training/evaluation separation, quantized estimands and continuous support declarations. Smooth full support alone does not establish IID sampling, finite MI or estimator validity. |

## Preservation and review

The index binds the source commits, Git modes, blob OIDs, lengths, raw SHA-256 digests,
appearances at both revisions, bundle identity and prerequisite. The isolated recovery check
compared both commit identities, tree objects, messages and all twenty-three distinct document
images. This is a bounded two-commit recovery, not a full-history clone or an authenticity
attestation. Local recovery alone does not authorize deleting an unverified remote or worktree.

Two model reviews wrote their initial mathematical critiques before receiving each other's
conclusions; the integrating author inspected the sources and adjudicated the findings. The
reviews share a host, project definitions, source custody and model class. They do not establish
human, institutional or data independence. All eighteen tip documents were parsed and rendered;
five representative renders were individually inspected in the first visual pass. Full visual,
accessibility, plotting and font-rendering qualification remains open.

The decision compared ten routes: retain the branch; delete its drafts; merge both commits;
activate eighteen unmodified assets; edit the historical originals; archive only the tip;
archive all distinct images and the two-commit recovery bundle; recover every generator and
rerun all studies; revise only source-bound conceptual diagrams; or replace explanations with
simpler MI/CMI examples. Exact archival custody with the explicit correction list is selected
for this maintenance milestone. Active publication requires a separate source-bound repair and
visual review. Deletion loses unique examples; bulk activation carries the identified errors;
tip-only custody loses five predecessor images and commit history.

role; alphabet; support; supported anchor; units; empirical versus population law; quantized

cumulative versus atom; signed versus component value; full versus partial lattice; lattice
cardinality; Boolean versus realized-event domain; implication reflection; key covariance;
