## Object and decision record

The question is whether the already accepted finite SAT-projection finding can
be published with a small portable reader, exact original data and explicit
limits. This helps another reader inspect the 34 assignments without access to
machine-specific original capture records. The object and definitions remain
those identified by [the CLI guide](README.md), the exact encoder and unchanged
checker. There is no new PID quantity or defining paper for this project tool.

The implementation edge is a three-line public-storage adapter in the original
controls plus a new reader. The reader calls the unchanged checker and requires
exact saved output bytes. The prospective reader's permissive JSON parsing and
mixed equality were repaired before portable execution; duplicate keys and
numeric/Boolean coercions receive finite controls. The pre-repair candidate is
preserved separately. This repair does not change the original checker, its
454 controls, original outputs or the mathematical finding.

Evidence consists of exact-byte inventory, source review, finite execution and
advisory model review. No human scientific review, institutional endorsement,
independent custody, authenticated authorship, blind selection or universal
parser theorem is claimed. Encoder-to-checker correspondence is limited to the
stated 34 exposed projections; paper-to-specification, original formal Finset
transport, compiled Rust/binary64 and estimand/application edges remain open.
No application or PID choice is recommended by this publication.
