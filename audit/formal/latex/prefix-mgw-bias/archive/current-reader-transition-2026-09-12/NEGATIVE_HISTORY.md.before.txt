## URI action reader rejection

The first portable full build reproduced the reviewed 370,867-byte PDF exactly, then failed at the URI-action reader. Each of its 17 HTTPS action dictionaries contains `/Type /Action`; the reader incorrectly required only `/S` and `/URI`. The failed stage stopped after one started build and 21 primary commands. No summary ran and no complete reproduction was accepted. The correction requires the exact three-key Action shape and retains the URI inventory, forbidden-key checks and raw PDF identity. The original failed run remains evidence; a source correction alone supplies no production credit.
