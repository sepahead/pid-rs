### Why carefully typed extensions matter

Many high-impact PID failures are object-identity failures rather than arithmetic failures: using
an estimator outside its support assumptions, treating KSG MI as a PID, moving a categorical MGW
result into an Ehrlich continuous route, relabeling a quantized proxy as the paper functional,
clamping signed atoms before an identity subtraction, or mixing training-fitted, same-sample,
permutation, and surrogate roles. A bare floating-point value cannot prevent any of these errors.

A scientific result type should therefore carry, where applicable: object/estimand identity, PID
family, support/domain, units, sample and fitting role, estimator versus evaluator identity,
method/source revision, assumptions, artifact verification/invalidation plus links to separate
evidence and claim records, warnings/abstention, resource budget, and admissible interpretation.
Its value has three distinct layers:

- **Potential scientific error prevention:** strong in mechanism, but not yet quantified, because
  invalid transfers can become explicit or unrepresentable and a result can retain the quantity it
  actually estimates.
- **Potential engineering and ecosystem composition:** strong in mechanism, but not yet evaluated
  for usability or cross-package error rates, because Rust, Python, run-log, resampling, and
  downstream consumers can exchange structured results without guessing what an unlabeled number
  means.
- **Scientific novelty:** not automatic. A type system becomes a potential methods/software-paper
  contribution only when its formal composition rules, prevented counterexamples, usability, and
  cross-package behavior are evidenced; adding type names alone is not a new PID or theorem.

This pattern generalizes beyond PID to causal estimands, posterior summaries, uncertainty
intervals, surrogate tests, imaging transforms, and action-conditioned forecasts. The transferable
principle is: **an unlabeled number is not a self-interpreting or safely transportable quantitative
scientific result; its object identity, assumptions, units, production route, and admissible
interpretations must travel with it.** Types still do not establish
paper-to-definition correspondence, estimator consistency/calibration, numerical correctness,
kernel soundness, or application validity; those require separate proofs, certificates,
experiments, and review.
