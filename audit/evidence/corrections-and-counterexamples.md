## Related corrections and checked limits

- **Robot measurements.** The donor's LP2 and LP3 tasks assign different sets of class labels to the same 47 recorded transfer cases. Force and torque are measured channels. A shared source table fixes the source-only quantities. The class labels enter all target-dependent terms. [Donor experiment](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4615-5725-8_23) · [Study and row comparison](../research/robot-wrist-slope-signs/EXPOSITION.md)
- **Angular approximation.** In the specified four-angle density model, hold the proper terms exact and require relative error strictly below one part per million. At radius 0.30 one axis passes and another fails. From 0.39 through 1 every direction fails. A later proof puts the last passing threshold strictly between 0.340315168725 and 0.340315168726. A distinct finite-grid repair restores eight amplitude pairs on the radius 0.90 boundary. [Earlier study](../research/angular-unit-boundary/EXPOSITION.md) · [Directional theorem](../research/angular-ray-monotonicity/EXPOSITION.md) · [Grid correction](../research/angular-quadrature-grid/EXPOSITION.md)
- **Strict neighbour counts.** The Kraskov, Stögbauer and Grassberger mutual-information estimator and the continuous Ehrlich shared-exclusions estimator use different neighbourhood rules. The attainable count pairs and whole-sample means have separate results. The ten-page report gives the assumptions and the five-row measured example. [Geometry, proof and examples](../research/strict-neighbour-count-geometry/README.md)
- **Finite categorical certificates.** The four inverse statements for a quoted state field hold under the specified byte grammar. The six complete certificates use a separate end-to-end check. The general signed rounding bound and the sharper result for returned coordinates from tables with at most 1,024 occupied rows have different premises. Two-row examples attain two output grid units. [State field](../research/categorical-state-token/README.md) · [Signed logarithms](../research/signed-log-certificate-enclosure/EXPOSITION.md)

- **Solver and library evidence.** Satisfiability of a background condition does not prove that a submitted statement encodes the intended theorem.
