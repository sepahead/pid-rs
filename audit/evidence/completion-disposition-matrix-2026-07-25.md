- `active-separate-claim`: implementation is permitted only through the named frozen claim packet;
- `deferred-research`: scientifically meaningful, but not closable by a responsible bounded change
  in this run;
- `external-blocker`: closure requires a consumer, human reviewer, calibration campaign, hardware,
  or authority process outside `pid-rs`;
- `rejected`: the recommendation is obsolete, category-confused, prohibited, or unsupported.

- all of `pid-rs` or any compiled binary is formally verified;

- an exact empirical zero/sign is a population or statistical conclusion;
- support, independence, ergodicity, no drift, or application validity was inferred from rows;
- software-identity equality proves compatibility, authenticity, or scientific validity;
- a consumer pin or passing library test qualifies a consumer;
- PID creates, widens, or authorizes an action; or
