## Formal statement boundary

Let `count` be a natural-valued function on the complete finite key type for exactly two possibly
heterogeneous finite source alphabets and one finite target alphabet. Assume
`0 < totalCount count`; zero-count complete keys remain permitted. Local logarithms and weighted
sums are restricted to `positiveSupport count`.

The fixed cumulative order is `[C1,C2,C12,CR]`; the fixed atom order is `[U1,U2,S,R]`; the fixed
component order is informative, misinformative, signed net. The concrete transform is

```text
R  = CR
U1 = C1  - CR
U2 = C2  - CR
S  = C12 - C1 - C2 + CR.
```

Its zeta inverse reconstructs the four cumulatives. Both compositions are identities over an
additive commutative group. Coordinate exchange is formula equivariance on an arbitrary four-entry
cumulative function; it transports no source types, keys, counts, laws, or events.

After specialization to the normalized nonnegative empirical law, the module proves:

- finite positive-mass averaging commutes with the concrete Möbius transform;
- every one of the 24 cumulative/atom coordinates equals its exact count expression;
- each coordinate equals `(1/N) * log(product)` for a positive real product;
- the real product is the cast of an exact rational product; and
- positive, negative, and zero are respectively equivalent to the product being above, below, or
  equal to one.

Generic real-valued `law` parameters in the algebraic layer denote support-restricted weighted
sums. They acquire an empirical-average interpretation only in the empirical-law specialization.
