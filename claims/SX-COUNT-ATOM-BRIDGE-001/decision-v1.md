## Adjudication

Decision: **hold for integration; mathematically coherent candidate, not yet accepted assurance**.

The candidate source presents a coherent extension of the completed count/event bridge. It fixes
the complete two-source coordinate surface for three components, proves the concrete Möbius/zeta
algebra, proves that finite empirical averaging commutes with the transform, lifts the exact-count
logarithm formulas from cumulatives to atoms, and reduces every coordinate's exact sign and zero to
a positive product comparison.

This is not a rejection of the mathematics. The hold records missing evidence layers: exact
kernel/checker binding, semantic negative controls, repository-wide provenance and scope binding,
deterministic PDF generation and visual inspection, and independent review.

## Candidate strengths

- The 24-coordinate type and order are explicit rather than inferred from field names.
- The node and atom orders match the intended two-source categorical layout and are not conflated.
- Möbius and zeta transforms are proved inverse over an additive commutative group.
- Source-labelled coordinate exchange is explicit and fixes joint, synergy, and redundancy
  positions; it is not a typed source-data transport theorem.
- Informative, misinformative, and signed-net components are all carried through the same algebra.
- Net subtraction is proved at local, averaged-cumulative, and averaged-atom levels.
- Pointwise inversion and averaged inversion are proved equal.
- Positive-support conditions are inherited from the completed event/count bridge.
- Every cumulative and atom receives both a real positive product and an exact rational
  counterpart.
- Positive, negative, and zero equivalences are uniform over the coordinate type.

## Reasons acceptance is withheld

1. The current source has not yet been accepted through the repository's pinned Lean replay and
   exact source/declaration inventory.
2. The future gate has not yet demonstrated failure on meaningful order, coefficient, event,
   weighting, product, sign, and scope changes.
3. The new formalization has not yet been integrated into the method catalog, assurance registry,
   public limitations, formal maps, and changelog.
4. The paired LaTeX report has not yet been rendered, text-extracted, page-inspected, and checked
   for deterministic reproduction.
5. Independent reviewers have not yet adjudicated the mathematical, provenance, implementation,
   and document boundaries.

## Residual decision boundary

Even after the pending gates pass, the bounded claim must continue to exclude:

- a theorem that informative or misinformative atoms are universally nonnegative;
- a row-to-count or bytes-to-count refinement;
- Rust `NODES2`, `invert2`, accumulation, atom-field, or binary64 refinement;
- standalone-certifier, Python, compiler, runtime, or resource refinement;
- statistical and population conclusions;
- continuous estimators and other PID measures; and
- three-source or general higher-source lattices.

The exact sign equivalences are not a substitute for component nonnegativity: they state how a
sign follows from the exact product, not which side of one the product must occupy.

## Acceptance conditions

The decision may change to bounded acceptance only when every pending K1, K2, D1, and R1 item in
[`obligations.md`](obligations.md) is closed with replayable evidence and no residual boundary is
widened. Until then, public references must label the claim preliminary.
