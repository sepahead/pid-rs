# Proposed decision for SX-CERTIFIED-AVERAGED-PID3-001, revision 1

## Decision

**Disposition: proposed.**

**Target evidence label: no accepted evidence.**

Revision 1 freezes a prospective exact-product and directed-interval assurance target for 108
averaged coordinates of one finite empirical three-source categorical
Makkeh--Gutknecht--Wibral shared-exclusions PID. It does not yet support the target implication,
even conditionally.

The decision is no-go for:

- an accepted 108-coordinate certificate;
- “formally verified SxPID3”;
- exact or interval refinement of current compiled Rust;
- population, estimator, calibration, or uncertainty claims;
- consumer qualification; and
- release-critical reliance on the external audit's Python output.

## What is accepted now

The following narrow negative or finite statements are retained independently of the target
decision:

1. exact small count tables distinguish OR across branches from AND, AND within masks from OR, and
   target-restricted from unrestricted event counts;
2. positional comparison fails between the certificate order and current specialized/general
   implementation orders;
3. uniform weighting of distinct support keys differs from empirical-count weighting;
4. an exact averaged signed-net SxPID3 atom can be negative;
5. a named binary64 mirror returns a nonzero residual for a mathematical exact zero;
6. a nonempty atom expression can have exact multiplicative product one;
7. the bounded binary domain contains 20,348 labeled count vectors, 20,164 primitive rational
   laws, and would require 2,197,584 verdicts for a complete 108-coordinate replay; and
8. the external alleged independent verifier shares its load-bearing event/count implementation
   with the primary mirror.

These are evidence labels `counterexample`, `exact finite arithmetic result`, or `diagnostic
observation` as specified in [evidence-matrix.md](evidence-matrix.md). None is a population theorem
or a proof of the prospective verifier.

## Why the external report is not accepted blindly

The external report made valuable discoveries, especially the exact-zero residual and
floating-evaluation-order sensitivity. Critical source inspection also found:

- the reported 1,098,792 values cover 54 rather than 108 coordinates;
- 20,348 count vectors are not 20,348 distinct laws;
- the second script imports the primary event/count core; and
- neither route binds current compiled Rust.

The corrected intake remains useful bounded diagnostic evidence. Its self-description as an
independent route and any implication of full 108-coordinate coverage are rejected.

## Required closure

All five programs in [routes.md](routes.md) must close:

1. primary-source and exact combinatorial semantics;
2. dual formal semantics, including both concrete Lean and independently encoded SMT;
3. exact products and independent directed magnitude;
4. compiled Rust keyed refinement; and
5. adversarial replay, immutable provenance, and final adjudication.

The detailed obligations in [obligations.md](obligations.md) remain open. In particular, generic
log algebra, a hard-coded $18\times18$ inverse, a 20,348-table Python replay, or model agreement
cannot substitute for the missing event-to-parser-to-verifier-to-binary chain.

## Residual scientific boundary

Even a future successful decision would certify a deterministic empirical categorical functional
for one accepted table under an explicit computing trust boundary. It would not prove:

- that shared exclusions is the unique or universally preferred PID;
- population consistency, unbiasedness, coverage, or power;
- validity for dependent, drifting, continuous, singular, or mixed-dimensional laws;
- equivalence of a quantized law and its unquantized source;
- causal or operational interpretation for a downstream decision; or
- correctness of another PID definition.
