# Claim SX-CERTIFIED-AVERAGED-PID3-001, revision 1

## Record status

This packet was created on 2026-07-26 as a prospective specification after an external audit was
reviewed critically against the paper-defined event semantics and the current Rust source. The
audit is input to the work plan, not an authority and not an independent acceptance route.

The disposition is **proposed, not conditionally supported**. The exact revision-1 mathematical
target is frozen here, but all five closure programs, their cross-bindings, and the final
adjudication remain open. No certificate or current executable output may cite this revision as an
accepted assurance result. See [decision.md](decision.md).

## Claim class and scientific identity

The categorical shared-exclusions functional is paper-defined by Makkeh, Gutknecht, and Wibral,
*Introducing a differentiable measure of pointwise shared information*, Physical Review E 103,
032149 (2021), arXiv:2002.03356. The canonical count-table transport, natural-log specialization,
18-key serialization order, 108-coordinate certificate, resource policy, independent-verification
report, mutation program, and assurance decision are project-defined.

This packet does not define a new PID measure. It certifies neither Williams--Beer $I_{\min}$ nor
any continuous estimator.

## Frozen object

- Exactly three finite categorical sources $S_1,S_2,S_3$ and one finite categorical target $T$.
- One finite empirical count table with positive total integer mass and one row per distinct
  supported joint realization.
- Definition revision
  `makkeh-gutknecht-wibral-2021-empirical-sxpid3-nats-v1`.
- Natural-logarithm units (nats). The paper's base-2 values are multiplied by $\ln 2$.
- The source masks, 18 antichain keys, redundancy order, event DNF, target restriction, and zeta
  orientation in [conventions.md](conventions.md).
- Exactly 108 averaged coordinates: 18 cumulatives and 18 atoms for each of informative,
  misinformative, and net.
- Exact positive-rational products for zero and strict sign, and independently checked outward
  intervals for exact-real magnitude.
- The versioned schemas and resource-policy identifiers in [bindings.md](bindings.md).

These objects do not change during one accepted verification.

## Prospective target implication

Let $B$ be bytes accepted under the canonical count-table schema and let $X=\mathrm{decode}(B)$
be their unique empirical count-vector semantics. For each coordinate identity $j$ in the frozen
108-coordinate order, [conventions.md](conventions.md) defines an exact real

$$
F_j(X)=\frac{1}{N}\log R_j(X),\qquad R_j(X)\in\mathbb Q_{>0}.
$$

Let an accepted certificate contain a normalized outward interval $I_j=[L_j,U_j]$ and an exact
product decision for $R_j$. The target implication for a future independent verifier is:

If the independently bound verifier returns the single terminal status `verified` for $(B,C)$,
then, under its explicit trusted-runtime assumptions, every one of the 108 intervals contains
the independently reconstructed exact-real categorical SxPID3 value,

$$
\forall j\in\{1,\ldots,108\},\quad L_j\le F_j(X)\le U_j,
$$

and each reported exact decision is `zero`, `positive`, or `negative` exactly when
$R_j=1$, $R_j>1$, or $R_j<1$.

Acceptance must additionally establish all of the following rather than trusting certificate
text:

1. strict schema, canonical-byte, identity-order, and digest bindings;
2. independent reconstruction of all event masses, 54 cumulative products, the semantic zeta
   matrix, its exact inverse, and all 54 atom products;
3. exact equality of reconstructed and certificate product objects;
4. independent rational-log intervals that are **subsets** of certificate intervals, not merely
   overlapping them;
5. parser, operation, memory, term, exponent, and projected-bit preflight before the corresponding
   expensive parse, scan, allocation, multiplication, or exponentiation;
6. exact sign/zero decisions by comparison of positive rationals with one, never by tolerance or
   by interval endpoint sign alone; and
7. successful completion of every mandatory route and critical-cut gate in
   [routes.md](routes.md), with no partial-output interpretation.

This is a target statement, not a current theorem about an executable.

## Domain separation

The per-input mathematical definition permits arbitrary finite categorical alphabets and positive
integer counts on finite observed support. The mandatory exhaustive qualification corpus is much
narrower: labeled binary $S_1,S_2,S_3,T$, the fixed 16-cell order, and totals $1\le N\le5$. It
contains 20,348 labeled count vectors but only 20,164 primitive rational laws. A future complete
108-coordinate replay has 2,197,584 verdicts. Results on that corpus are bounded software evidence
only. They do not turn the per-input implication into an exhaustive theorem over arbitrary finite
alphabets.

The proposed report covers averaged coordinates. Pointwise values reported by an external
differential corpus are not certified by this claim revision.

## Explicit premises

1. The frozen union-of-source-conjunctions event and target restriction faithfully specialize the
   cited categorical shared-exclusions definition.
2. Changing from $\log_2$ to $\ln$ changes units only, by the positive factor $\ln 2$.
3. The canonical input decoder denotes exactly one finite count table.
4. The independent verifier implements the frozen formulas, exact integer/rational arithmetic,
   lattice semantics, rigorous logarithm enclosure, and preflight ordering.
5. The executed source and dependencies are the bytes bound by the verification report.
6. Trusted proof kernels, solvers, language runtimes, integer libraries, parsers, hashes,
   filesystems, compilers, and operating systems behave within the explicitly recorded boundary.
7. A consumer accepts only a complete terminal result for the same input, claim revision, and
   source manifest.

These premises remain premises. Agreement among implementations does not convert the common
paper-to-formula interpretation into an independent proof.

## Falsifiers

The prospective implication is false if any accepted result exhibits one of these conditions:

1. an exact target value lies outside its reported interval;
2. a strict sign or zero label disagrees with the exact rational product;
3. the event uses conjunction between antichain branches, disjunction inside a branch, or omits
   the target intersection;
4. source-mask bits or antichain keys are permuted without a corresponding semantic relabeling;
5. $Z_{ij}$ is transposed relative to “row cumulative, column atom” semantics;
6. a non-antichain, padded-zero key, duplicate row, noncanonical count, or duplicate JSON member is
   accepted;
7. equal supported realizations receive uniform rather than empirical-count weight;
8. informative and misinformative parts are swapped, added, clamped, or combined with the wrong
   quotient;
9. base-2 numbers are labeled as nats or the $1/N$ factor is omitted;
10. a nonempty product equal to one is classified by syntactic term emptiness alone;
11. preflight occurs only after unbounded products, powers, allocations, or parser work;
12. an interval that only overlaps the independent interval is accepted;
13. a certificate, source, lattice, schema, or claim revision is changed and merely resealed under
    an unbound digest; or
14. the bounded corpus is described as distinct probability laws, arbitrary-alphabet coverage,
    pointwise certification, or compiled-Rust refinement.

The five named retained failure records are mandatory regression objects:

- [event connectives and target restriction](failures/event-connective-and-target-mutations.md);
- [mask and array order](failures/mask-and-array-order.md);
- [empirical-count weighting](failures/count-weighting.md);
- [negative signed-net atom](failures/negative-net-atom.md); and
- [exact-zero and nonsyntactic-product-one boundaries](failures/exact-zero-and-product-one.md).

Passing them proves sensitivity to those faults only.

## Non-solutions

None of the following closes this claim:

- copying the two-source certifier and changing four nodes to eighteen;
- checking only $MZ=ZM=I$ without entrywise order semantics;
- two programs importing the same event/count/lattice implementation;
- comparing the specialized and generic Rust APIs positionally;
- comparing rounded floats with a tolerance to decide exact zero or sign;
- using high precision without directed bounds and an independent containment check;
- treating a producer exit code, digest match, solver `sat`, or proof build alone as end-to-end
  acceptance; or
- renaming a bounded mirror “formal verification” or “independent reproduction.”
