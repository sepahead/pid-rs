# Retained failure: arithmetic-route label and maximum-tie wording

Revision 1 labeled the `16`-epsilon, `764`-asymmetry route “compensated direct four-term harmonic
arithmetic.” An independent replay shows that signature belongs to plain left-associated
arithmetic. Neumaier four-term reduction instead has `8` eps, zero asymmetries, and 39 maximum
ties. The selected sorted range has `8` eps, zero asymmetries, and 40 maximum ties.

Consequently, `(4096,1,2048,2048)` is the first maximum-attaining cell, not “the worst cell” in a
unique sense. The complete selected-route tie set is retained in
`route-memo-exact-numerics-erratum-v2.md`.

The bad label did not select the production route and therefore does not refute its bounded
signature. It does refute the accuracy of the route evidence and must remain visible. Freezing the
40-way multiplicity supplies a regression that distinguishes the selected route from the
39-tie Neumaier alternative even though both share the same maximum and swap count.
