## Exceptional cases and residual obligations

- `k=0`, `k>=n`, zero arguments, invalid counts, zero radii, and nonunique kth shells remain
  caller errors or abstentions; the harmonic helper's debug assertions are not public validation.
- Exact-zero local terms remain numerical values, not statistical null declarations.
- The 80-digit Decimal zero residual prevents an ULP-from-reference gate.
- A source-symmetric local arithmetic helper does not by itself prove every enclosing estimator
  path is source-permutation equivariant.
- The Lean/SMT routes do not prove analytic digamma truth. `M1` is a typed shared premise at their
  boundary; only Lean defines the harmonic finite sum, while SMT deliberately leaves its values
  uninterpreted.
- Neither formal route contains neighbor geometry, binary64, Rust, shared-exclusions event
  semantics, estimator consistency, support, calibration, or consumer objects.
- The finite corpus does not cover every large-`n`, near-cancelling tuple or non-tested floating
  environment.
