the same model with $b=0$, $a\ne0$ is a counterexample.

## Application routes, not demonstrated benefits

Applications are not limited to sensors. Each route needs a declared
source/target law and task comparison before a PID interpretation.

| Area | Defensible next test | Critical assumption |
|---|---|---|
| Neural activity and imaging | Compare frozen preprocessing and held-out decoding with MI/CMI and task loss | Consistent trial labels; separate subjects/runs; dependence-aware uncertainty |
| Robotics, drones and embodied intelligence | Compare pre-action histories with future outcomes under matched actions | No future leakage; matched episode/action law; failures remain in the denominator |
| VLA and world models | Audit fixed vision/language/state groups against declared outcomes | Distinguish observed futures from model futures and constant context |
| Crebain/NCP | Offline aligned-record or target-coarsening audit | Fixed encoding, missingness and target; transport supplies no control authority |
| Prisoma | Fresh episode-level comparison against constant, persistence and action-conditioned predictors | Preserve existing null and same-row/singleton negatives; no reuse as confirmation |
| Flux Action3 | Compare registered rollout outcomes alongside returned action plans | Action predictions are not an observed-future outcome oracle |

The angular result is useful as a controlled circular-variable reference.
Applying it to recordings requires evidence for its very specific density,
not just angular coordinates or a learned embedding. Categorical applications
use their own MGW estimand; this continuous example cannot certify them.
